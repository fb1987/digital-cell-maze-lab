## Matched geometry

Twelve independently seeded contexts, 25 by 25, each with eight D4 poses, two
goals, straight and bent source arms and prefix lengths 1,2,4,6,8. Source remains
an endpoint by truncating the extended arm. Goal arms (6–9 edges), goals,
downstream graph and ten isolated clutter cells remain identical across prefix
length and shape within each context/family/pose. Bends begin after three edges;
lengths 1/2 intentionally duplicate across shape labels and are correlated.
Bent direction alternates by context; translations, tails, cycle depth and
clutter vary. This restricted orthogonal grammar is a diagnostic, not an ordinary
maze distribution or a fully factorial test of arbitrary bends.

Five branching graphs: fork; an extra two-cell dead end at distance 2 or 4 on
one goal arm; real opposite-arm cycles attached at distance 2 or 4 from the first
junction. Cycle depth is fixed within a context at 3–5. Varying cycle attachment
also changes alternative length and open-cell count: only the dead-end contrast
moves a branch while preserving the count. These are not pure signal-distance
interventions. All graphs retain the same endpoints and unique shortest routes.
Each cycle also has a verified legal longer output raster. Two matched pruned
corridors and a fork disconnected at its junction accompany every variant.

Per model: 9,600 branched inputs / 4,800 goal pairs, 1,920 corridors and 1,920
no-path cases = 13,440. Five first-distance levels are matched observations,
not independent experiments. Shape labels at lengths 1/2 duplicate inputs;
report them separately and do not claim extra replication. No candidate-dependent
rejection, sampling, or final-driven adaptation.
