# M13: broader mutation under persistent retention pressure

Authorized by the user's Proceed after M12. This is a new bounded experiment,
not an extension or reselection of any completed history. Random-origin discovery
remains central. No known solver, backtrace, recombination or oracle enters inference.

Question: can broader generic variation escape the limited C6 repertoire while
preserving acquired skills under the same explicit retention bank? M12 established
a descriptive retention benefit, but no integrated or deeper-route solver. Its
lineages could edit C6 only. This experiment compares mutation POLICY PACKAGES:
focused 50% C6 generic edits / 50% final C6 writer-gate edits, versus the unchanged
M1 generic mutation operator across all eight blocks. The latter has no extra
gate-edit mixture. Thus a difference is not isolated causality of block location.
