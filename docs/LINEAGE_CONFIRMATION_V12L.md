claims; rescue is an external intervention, not evolved regulation or proof of

## Decision rules fixed before main generation

Primary lineage confirmation: replicate-two target completes all sixteen paired
poses/locations as a fork in at least four independent templates spanning ALL
three goal forms, and exceeds its actual G entry ancestor's total complete pairs.
Report exact target/parent/entry/frozen comparisons, per-family/prefix/location
counts and corridor retention; passing this narrow screen is not general solving
or a claim that the immediate mutation was causal. Replicate one is a secondary
fixed comparison with the same descriptive screen, not a replacement primary.

External harmful-exposure screen: for an old Branch target, nonlocal zeroing gives
positive net pair gains in at least four independent templates spanning all three
goal forms, with no loss of any normally successful pair or corridor on the fixed
mechanism subset. Report far-prefix opportunities and retention explicitly;
absence of normal far successes makes retention vacuous. Parent/child results are
related observations, not independent replicates.

Only if primary normal lineage confirmation AND at least one old Branch rescue
screen pass does L recommend a separate heritable-exposure calibration. This is
not authorization to extend L or launch a large search. Otherwise preserve all
lineages and diagnose the failed transfer dimension before designing a new pilot.
Fresh-origin discovery remains open; unchanged zero-pair pressure is not a useful
next test. A subsequent fresh-origin calibration needs new elementary paired
cases and measurable reproductive selection before allocating 60–90 minutes.
