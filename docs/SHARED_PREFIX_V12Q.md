## Design and population

Six pools of96: carry/fresh ×3 search RNG replicates. Every carry pool protects
nine exact saved genomes: O route depths9/16, reach1/reach2 depth16, four original
M selected anchors and O unmutated resample. Remaining slots use original M
founders of that replicate, ordered by SHA256(identity), skipping duplicates.
Carry replicates share anchors and routing ancestry; not independent origins.
Fresh pools are new rich random64–128-gene draws, no carried anchors. Independent
seed namespaces preserve full founder/search RNG. Capacity ceiling256 unchanged.

Each pool is copied into current, mixed and resample arms, including identical
search RNG. Current and mixed evolve; resample uses the mixed stimulus schedule.
The sole changed factor between evolution arms is paired-task source mixture:
current100% prefix0; mixed50% prefix0/50% prefix1. Twelve inputs/generation/model:
two seeded contexts, each two goal-paired forks, two pruned corridors, two isolated-
goal disconnected inputs. Corridor/no-path controls always prefix0 and identical
between arms. Downstream fork layout and goals remain fixed as source moves.
Training arm lengths2/3, straight/turning goals, straight five-edge stem,19×19
boards, eight isolated clutter sites. Shape cycles every4generations; pose changes
every4generations, covering all32 shape/pose combinations every32generations.
Each generation has fresh clutter contexts shared by every condition. Route shapes
repeat; clutter contexts and poses are not independent algorithm discoveries.

Existing M paired-first fitness/lexicase is common: paired shortest, individual
shortest, legal, correctNP-minus-falseNP, bidirectional thin-output partial credit.
No partial output meets a success gate. Common24elites preserve all protected
