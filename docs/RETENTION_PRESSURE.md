## Hypothesis and comparison

M9 found a two-mutation conditional C6 regulator with full corridor/disconnection
retention and half the specialist pairs, but fresh ordinary cases exposed three
parental losses despite equal success totals. Its third edit recovered the other
specialist pairs while losing corridors. The hypothesis here is that broader
rotating ordinary training exposure can reduce such substitutions and improve
integration. This is a curriculum comparison, not a new runtime or isolated test
of regulation. It does not assume greater exposure will solve the deeper barrier.

Three paired histories, six populations of96. Each pair starts with identical
ordered genomes, root labels and RNG; pairs have independent future RNG streams.
Twelve common protected references: all ten M9 sources plus exact M9 regulation0
partial g62/b51 and specialist g63/b27. Remaining84 slots alternate42 exact clones
of each of those two gated lineages. Every source genome and inherited record is
preserved. M10 mutation depth zero means zero new M10 births, not new random origin.
Old populations and journals remain unchanged. These are related histories.

Each round retains44 and creates52:7 ordinary full random immigrants and45
one-opportunity children with unrestricted lexicase survivor parent choice.
BOTH arms use exactly the M9 regulation mixture: half uniformly mutate final C6
gate to ALWAYS or a valid earlier register; half existing generic M1 mutation with
destination forced to C6 after consuming its normal block draw. Other blocks and
ports unchanged except generic copying/rebinding into C6. No-op proposals consume
births. Neither arm uses M9's global operator. No breeding or survival lineage quota.
The sole arm difference is ordinary training exposure, specified below.

## Common selection, neutrality and nomination

Fitness is computed externally from independently graded rasters. Both arms use
the same parent-retention/specialist-coverage ranking. For each candidate, compare
its current-case capabilities with the fixed quota parent and child. Rank by no
false no-path, minimum retention over nonempty parent complete-skill sets,
fraction of child-only complete pairs retained (zero when absent on the panel),
mean nonempty parent retention, then the old domain-balanced optimal/valid/partial
rank. This is the unchanged M9 scoring rule in both M10 arms. No success is inferred from a vacuous denominator.

Twelve protected entries remain exact; eight merit survivors, up to12 uncovered-case
specialists and up to18 genotype-distinct variants sharing finite-panel behavior
with merit entries fill the44 slots, with lexicase for any remainder. These are
capacity limits, not additive guarantees. Neutrality is only observed on the
current batch. Mutations need not improve or pass integration to survive/breed.
Archive16 retains four ranked entries and complementary coverage.

Assessment at rounds0,32,64,96,128 and any earlier normal soft/call stop. Three
fixed nomination roles: integrated (above ranking), upstream-exploratory (new
nonforkprefix2+ coverage beyond protected union), ordinary (old ordinary-domain
rank). Round0 nominees are frozen references. No role switching on final data.

Exactly96x48 nominal train queries and115x392 nominal assessment queries per
population. Assessment slots unused by live/archive/frozen identities are padded
with nonselectable exact A0 queries. Physical genotype/query dedup is explicit.
Equal nominal evaluations and mutation births do not imply equal CPU/work.
