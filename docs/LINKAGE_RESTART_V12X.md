## Generic operator and homology

Each gene has an inherited immutable identity; replacements/operand changes retain
it, deletion removes it, swaps move it, insertions/duplicates receive new unique
identities. Initial unrelated founders do not acquire homology because rows look
alike. Historicald07/d12 have explicitly reconstructed shared U identities with
original106deleted; all other historical founders conservatively start separate
identity namespaces. This can understate old homology and is disclosed. Fresh
related descendants accumulate shared identities through actual recorded ancestry.

Crossover segments have2/4/8/16instructions in BOTH parents, bracketed by shared
identity anchors. Shared-locus order must agree; inverted pairs are ineligible.
No whole-program transfer, register/channel renaming or inferred solver code.
The new common pool permits different scratch write/store sets in both arms,
explicitly relaxing old S's restrictive interface. It is not an unchanged S test.
A segment must change at least one instruction row; transferred tags alone do not
count as a genotype change. Capacity remains unchanged by these equal-length swaps.

Dependency groups are backwards transitive closures from every persistent STORE's
value/gate scratch reads, including dependencies of their defining instructions.
Conditional definitions retain earlier possible writers; unconditional definitions
kill them. Groups from BOTH parents are united on inherited identities. A STORE
replaced by EQ in one parent therefore still carries the other parent's dependency
constraints. A proposed segment must include each intersected group in full; large
groups are rejected, never truncated or expanded beyond16instructions. This is
conservative within-tick scratch dependency, not certified functional modularity
or a complete across-tick persistent-channel dependency model.

All three sexual policies select length uniformly from available common-pool
lengths. Linked chooses uniformly among dependency-closed candidates at that length;
unlinked chooses among all candidates. Sham chooses a uniformly randomized subset
of the common candidates of exactly the same cardinality as the linked eligible
set, then uniformly within it. Linked and sham therefore have identical proposal
length and acceptance feasibility on the SAME parent pair/RNG snapshot. This
controls boundary restriction without following functional dependencies. All arms
consume the same ordering/ticket random draws even when falling back to mutation.
Actual marginal distributions can diverge with populations; report proposed and
accepted lengths, rejection reasons and successful ancestry explicitly. Retain
unlinked and mutation references for practical utility, not just linked-vs-sham.

Count activation only if the child retains at least TWO donor instructions whose
rows differed at the recipient's transferred positions, after its subsequent
mutation, and its genotype differs from the recipient. Identical rows with new
identities, single-instruction transfer, failed mating or mutation-only children
cannot satisfy this gate. This measures actual genetic transfer, not combined
functional abilities. Record both pre-mutation changes and retained donor changes.
