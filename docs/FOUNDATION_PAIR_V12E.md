# V12E: controlled comparison of completed-pair priority

Preregister and push before search. V12D's fresh multirange cohort acquired short
paired routes, largely two edges, without general solving or a causal nonlocal
advantage. Four validation champion replacements gained individual successes
while losing complete pairs. The present hypothesis is that prioritizing
complete shortest pairs can extend retained short-route behavior to new 3–8-edge
paired tasks. A failure is informative; no performance-based budget extension.

## Matched design and single selection change

Fork the six fresh-origin evolution populations at V12D generation 865 into
Current and Paired arms. Preserve their ordered 96 individuals, DNA, identities,
serial counters, exact RNG, histories, skills, competence and original frozen
controls. Give descendants new pressure-specific ID namespaces; their existing
parents keep ancestral IDs. Continue the six matching V12D resampling populations
under Current priority. Total: 18 conditions, local/evolved-range × two evolution
policies plus one resampling control × three replicates. No native cohort, known
solver, archived-champion injection, founder restart or cross-replicate transfer.

Current uses the unchanged global rank `(V,O,N,P,S,W)`. Paired uses
`(P,V,O,N,S,W)`: P is the number of pairs with BOTH ordinary emitted routes
shortest-correct; V/O are individual legal/shortest counts, N is correct no-path
minus false reachable claims, S is unchanged ordinary partial shaping and W is
the unchanged complete-batch cost tie-break. This ordering affects ranking,
validation nominees, elite/niche retention and the sorted parent pool. Per-case
lexicase fields are reordered from `(correct,optimal,pair,partial)` to
`(pair,correct,optimal,partial)`; epsilon remains .02 only on partial credit.
Because pair success implies both correctness fields, this field reordering
alone is logically redundant on a fixed ordered pool. Do not claim otherwise.
The global ordering can differ; verify it on the four saved V12D tradeoffs.

Both use unchanged one-edit mutations, 8 channels, 256 genes, 256 ticks,
4096 reads/site, 32768 dispatches/site, 12 retained elites/niches, 9 immigrants
and 75 descendants per generation. Resampling retains 12 and redraws 84; it
does not inherit mutated offspring. Mutation RNG and 10% random-parent rule
stay unchanged. Four validation nominees every eighth absolute generation.

To avoid confounding breeding pressure with final selection, **both arms keep
two common validation archives**: original rank and paired-first rank. The
primary final model uses the common paired-first archive in every arm;
original-rank selection is a labelled secondary analysis. Both archives start
from the same V12D checkpoint champion with its saved full validation rows.
Existing per-level archives and all historical data remain preserved; no
post-hoc mining/injection of earlier discarded validation champions. Policy
champions remain separately recorded. Strict rank improvements replace archives;
ties retain the earlier selection. Neither common archive changes reproduction.

## Freeze, fresh confirmation and fixed decision rule

Freeze and push final primary/common-original/checkpoint/original-frozen models
before generating confirmation cases. Also freeze common-paired selections at
additional generations 0,48,128,256,512 and final (omit boundaries beyond the
run). Evaluate these on the same corpus as a secondary correlated time course.

Five separate elementary bands: distance 1, exactly 2, 3–4, 5–8 and 9–16 edges
for BOTH goals. For each band/replicate generate three independent tree and
three cyclic base layouts, all eight dihedral poses, two goals each: 480 inputs
and 240 pairs/replicate. Bands 3–4 and 5–8 are primary transfer; exact-two is
retention. Prefix/tail constraints are fixed in `foundation_pair_data.BANDS`.
Primary, common-original, V12D checkpoint and original frozen selections see
all bands. Retain all secondary archives without selecting on final results.

A topology passes in at least two of three matched replicates only if the
SAME primary Paired genome satisfies every condition:

1. More complete shortest pairs across the 3–4/5–8 bands than each of Current's
   common-paired primary, the V12D checkpoint and continued resampling primary.
2. Those successes span at least two independent base layouts, with at least
   one layout solved in all eight paired poses.
3. At least 44/48 adjacent pairs and at least ceil(90% of the V12D checkpoint's
   exact-two pair count) on the same fresh inputs. Report the threshold and zero
   baselines explicitly; do not combine specialists to pass retention.

Report all individual/pair/group counts, both families, all bands, all replicates
and failed gate components. This is an exploratory screen across two topology
cohorts, without a significance claim. No automatic extension even if it passes.

Ordinary generalization retains 88 inputs/replicate: 72 reachable and 16
disconnected, including 9/17/33/65 trees/cyclic grids and rectangles. Save/exclude
all orbits but query the primary orientation. Use separate 1024-tick/8192-read/
131072-dispatch-per-site limits. Separate compiled BFS/A*/bit-wavefront and intact
wavefront controls; none enters evolution. Benchmark 30 randomized warm repeats,
bytes decoding through raster emission, production JIT signatures, static model
setup separate. Use the fixed first 17/33 tree/cyclic case per replicate and each
model's first non-adjacent elementary success. Wrong outputs get no speed credit.

## Preflight, audit and interpretation

Before launch test all fork identities and RNGs, Current parity with unchanged
search across nomination/validation, deterministic resume, the actual recorded
priority tradeoffs, lexicase's logical redundancy, validation/exclusion identity,
construction-only band/pose labels and gate boundaries. Preserve new fixtures.
Do not modify completed V12 sources/raw files. New auxiliary confirmation/audit/
report code may be prepared during search, hashed before its own outputs.

Independently grade all validation/final rasters, every training success and a
fixed 1/512 sample of other training records; verify every fitness calculation,
new mutation, ancestry, parent-pool membership, archive transition, shared task
schedule, RNG/population continuation and response/work bounds. Replay every
distinct final model/case and reference output. Record audit overhead separately.
Optional frozen-model success-conditioned pulse/nonlocal/both interventions
retain static goal flags, endpoint halting and ablated-read costs. They are
dependence diagnostics, not another accuracy assay or proof of topology benefit.

Save full states, raw data, a report, successful/failing raster figures and one
evidence-based next recommendation. Preserve random-origin discovery as an active
research track; no known solver insertion, final-case tuning or unbounded run.
