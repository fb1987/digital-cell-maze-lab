# V12A: calibration before the longer random-origin study

Protocol written before the study, following the research audit and the user's explicit goal authorization. This is a positive-control and repair experiment. It does not measure random-origin discovery. V1–V11 code/raw outputs stay unchanged.

The current eight-channel/256-gene runtime, pulse convention, mutation operator, and global halting contract are retained. Reference paths never enter inference. All known programs and descendants in this calibration remain in a separate namespace and directory and must never enter random-origin populations.

The task factory supplies four levels: source–goal distances 1, 2–4, 4–8, and 8–16. Paired goals share layout and source and require disjoint shortest exits at a common fork. Minimum common-prefix/tail distances are explicit in `foundation_data.LEVELS`. Level0 genuinely permits an elementary source-or-goal rule; it is not considered general solving. Both tree and cyclic grids are included. Each independent base layout is transformed through all eight rotations/reflections. Transforms are correlated tests, not eight independent discoveries. Every whole layout in an orbit must be absent from prior studies; validation and final groups are separate. A mixed reachable/unreachable group checks the no-path convention with varied source placement.

Fixture/preflight seed1210 is distinct from study seed2026101812. Fixtures are saved and excluded. All9,296 whole layouts through V11 are excluded. Calibration train, validation and final layouts will also be excluded from the later random-origin study.

The control panel has the intact wavefront program plus: a blocked west-return accumulation gate, a blocked parent-memory write gate, an output-store source redirected to an unused scratch register, two blocked directional return gates, and combined parent/output defects. Exact rows are saved. Run the panel with local/evolved topology and goal pulse off/on. Record ordinary completed outputs separately from observations at ticks8/16/32/64, with ordinary versus forced stopping. Probe-raster legality is explicitly diagnostic and never counted as ordinary route success. Count all normal/probe work.

The candidate learning condition evaluates ordinary outputs only. Fitness orders legal routes, shortest routes, correct-no-path utility, complete shortest goal pairs, then bounded partial output. Partial output measures progress of thin connected components from either endpoint; off-component pixels and branches are penalized. It does not score an internal channel or force development after halting. The formula is in `foundation_fitness.partial`; no specific oracle raster is rewarded. Goal/source distances used to grade outputs remain outside inference. A normal correct-exit metric is structural and is not automatically equivalent to older source-removal causal assays.

Repair three defects: west-return gate, output-register redirection, and two-return gates. Each has three independently seeded runs,64 organisms, at most128 generations, no random immigrants or intact founders, and1–6 edits from the unchanged bounded operator. Retain1/8 by best fitness and behavioral niches; the rest are mutated children using epsilon lexicase with10% random parent choice. All ancestry and concrete edit traces are saved. Training rotates fresh paired tasks through levels1–3, two pairs/four inputs per generation; transformations vary with generation. Validation uses six base groups/all eight transforms/96 inputs per replicate. Initial populations and up to four nominees every8 generations are validated. Exact successful validation repair stops that condition; otherwise stop at128 generations or60 seconds per condition, whichever occurs first. This wall limit is independent of performance. Save next population/RNG at each generation.

Freeze every selected model before constructing new confirmation groups. Confirmation repeats the validation distribution on six fresh base layouts per replicate, all eight transforms. Run selected and initial frozen controls plus BFS. Do not multiply shared layouts across variants into independent trials. A successful repair replicate requires at least95% shortest ordinary outputs,95% complete shortest pairs, and a gain of at least25 percentage points over its initial frozen control. The repair calibration passes if the intact panel is correct on all reachable/no-path inputs and at least two of the three damage types pass in at least two replicates. Failure diagnoses searchability; it does not establish that random evolution is impossible.

Inspect the timing/partial matrix in addition to the repair gate: a useful goal-connected partial trace should receive nonzero ordinary-output credit, and forced states must not enter selection. Unit checks cover geometry, symmetries, exclusion, runtime/normal-observer parity, trivial-rule limitations, checkpoint resumption, equal resampling response slots, and exact mutation traces. Independently audit masks, metrics, selected replays, sources, layouts and lineages before interpreting the result. Report calibration compute separately from any later60–90-minute study. A passing repair gate permits profiling/preregistering that study; it does not itself establish a random-origin solver.
