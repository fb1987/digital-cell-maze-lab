# General-solver research reboot

User-authorized goal, October 20, 2026: confirm useful deep-search discoveries,
then test whether generic modular organization and broader training improve
random-origin acquisition with retention. Preserve the completed campaign.
No known solver or oracle-derived inference component enters a random lineage.

## Sequence and decision boundaries

1. **Deep confirmation C1.** All 12 frozen nominees, all 288 launch organisms,
   and immediate parents of mutated nominees on identical untouched inputs.
   Historical references and lineage roots are contained in the launch panel.
   A random immigrant is its own root, never credited with evolved acquisition.
   Register source, panel, seeds, exclusion set, domains, metrics and budgets;
   push before confirmation generation. No training or reselection in C1.
2. **Generic modular representation and evolvability preflight.** Private
   working registers and explicit inter-block state connections; bounded state,
   work and genotype sizes. Do not install a distance wave, route backtrace,
   shortest-path test or solver-specific stages. Compare with the existing
   instruction representation. Establish semantic/software checks and realistic
   timing before registering the scientific pilot. Exact design remains open.
3. **Matched discovery pilot.** Separate representation and curriculum effects,
   retain an old-representation comparison, multiple independent random starts,
   unchanged/random references, explicit continued lineages where appropriate,
   balanced reachable/disconnected reporting and untuned confirmation. Match
   evaluation allocations and report actual work rather than pretending equal
   evaluation counts imply equal compute. Freeze pilot design before search.

C1 results may inform the explicitly C1-informed next phase, but never become its
confirmation cases. No unchanged extension of the 10,000-round campaign. Sexual
recombination and nonlocal signalling are deferred until functional transfer or
communication experiments justify them. One negative pilot cannot close the
random-origin direction. A new long run requires documented evidence about
acquisition with retention and generalization, not only a generation target.

## C1 frozen protocol (effective when registration is pushed)

- Seed 2026102009; discarded preflight seed 202610201009. All 952,219 prior whole
  layouts and all fixture orientations excluded. Reject any D4 orbit overlap.
- 12 fresh structured contexts in the existing 19x19 grammar, prefixes 0/1/2/4/8,
  forks/second junctions/alternative cycles where supported, four paired poses,
  corridor pose zero, prefix-zero disconnected pose zero. Structural variants
  and poses correlate; contexts are sampling units. New layouts, not a claim of
  novel structured topology.
- Ordinary domains: Wilson perfect11, perfect15, random9, random15,
  partition-disconnected9, partition-disconnected15; 32 bases/domain, two poses
  queried, all eight poses excluded. Perfect11/random9 match the final phase;
  size15 is size transfer. Random generation and disconnected partition biases
  are disclosed. Uniform open endpoints in perfect; reachable-component endpoint
  sampling in random. No route-length-based filtering or adaptive sample sizing.
- All candidates use unchanged local runtime, exposure7, 256 genes/ticks,
  4096 reads/site and32768 dispatches/site. Separate intact VM, compiled BFS,
  compiled A*, and bitwave controls on the identical queries. An intact-control
  budget failure on larger cases is reported, never filtered out.
- Report optimal/valid outputs, true and false disconnected decisions, paired
  success by graph/prefix, corridor retention, ordinary success by family/size,
  per-base pose consistency, gains/losses against exact roots, immediate parents,
  the best historical reference and the full launch-population success union.
  Always report losses and opportunity denominators; no oracle-selected ensemble.
- Descriptive acquisition gate for mutated nominees: >=8 new paired successes
  versus root across >=2 contexts; >=90% retention of root paired and corridor
  successes (empty root categories explicitly N/A); no increase in false no-path.
  Broad-transfer flag additionally requires >=4 newly solved ordinary bases
  versus root, with both queried poses correct, including >=1 perfect and >=1
  random base. No genome selection depends on these gates. Report gates for each
  nominee, not as independent replicate significance or policy-causal evidence.
- Candidate cap1,000,000 physical calls, charged work10^14; 10 workers; 1h session
  wall ceiling, 12GiB process-tree RSS, 2GiB new-run storage, 16GiB free floor.
  Guards checkpoint/drain tasks; do not bypass or silently extend. Raw receipts,
  timing and RNG-free immutable panel survive interruption. Deduplicate only
  exact physical genome+runtime models with identical query lists.
- Audit independent raster grades for every result, receipt/source/panel hashes,
  full nominee ancestry reconstruction, deterministic corpus regeneration, all
  reported comparisons/gates; replay one fixed hash-selected case in32 per
  physical model with native runtime and every baseline. This is a sampled
  inference replay, not a second VM. Preflight and audit cost reported separately.
- No latency/speedup claim. Complete and seal C1 before using it to guide phase2.
