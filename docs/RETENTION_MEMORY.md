# M12: matched persistent retention measurement during evolution

Authorized October 21, 2026 by “Proceed” after M11's recommendation. This is a
new bounded experiment. Preserve all prior sources, seals, populations, RNG
histories and journals. No old search is resumed and no known solver enters a
random-origin lineage. No new runtime, sexual recombination or oracle inference.

## Question and isolated comparison

M11 showed that its reference-conditioned banks detected ordinary-skill losses
in a fixed related pool more consistently than the current panels. M12 asks
whether that measurement helps evolution integrate skills. Calibration success
does not imply evolutionary success or broad maze solving.

Three paired histories, six populations of 96. Use the exact M10 founders:
12 protected reference genomes plus 42 clones each of M9 g62/b51 (gated partial)
and g63/b27 (gated specialist). Only identifiers and future RNG change. Within
each pair, ordered genomes, roots and initial RNG match. Across pairs, future
RNG differs; these are related histories, not independent origins. M12 mutation
depth counts only M12 births. M10/M11 outcomes do not promote a new founder.

The ONLY arm difference is the fixed ordinary-maze measurement: **current** uses
M11's unconditioned ordinary panel; **bank** uses its parent-successful panel.
Reuse all THREE exact M11 development panels, assigned by replicate, including
the original paired current/bank pairing. They were frozen before M11 confirmation.
No inspected M11 confirmation case is reused. Reuse is explicit training exposure,
not independent evidence. The source panels and provenance hashes are registered
before any new scientific generation. No bank is rebuilt, adapted, filtered or
expanded based on M12 performance. All original M11 layouts remain excluded from
NEW generation, while these specifically declared development cases are reused.

Each bank contains 24 ordinary bases, both poses: four each P11/P15/R9/R15 with
1–7 edges and P11/P15 with 8–15 edges, selected by the fixed quota parent. The
current counterpart also contains 24 bases/two poses, from its original M10-style
four strata. This is a comparison of conditioning, coverage and length/size mix
together, not identification of one causal ingredient. There is no 16+ bank quota.
M11's construction calls are prior additional cost, not free extra information.

## Cases and equal prospective budgets

Every round gives BOTH arms the same 48 fresh inputs: M10's rotating schedule of
18 grammar queries, 24 ordinary queries (12 bases/two opposite poses), and six
additional disconnected queries. All six populations share this fresh batch.
Use unchanged M10 data generation and its recorded rejection limits; any unused
control-arm draws that its generator also produces are excluded and recorded.
To these 48 fresh inputs, append the assigned panel's 48 fixed ordinary inputs:
**96 queries per organism per training round**, in both arms. These fixed inputs
participate in survivor scoring, coverage preservation, finite-panel neutrality
and lexicase parent choice. There is no additional hidden retention evaluation.
Report fresh and repeated presentations separately; repetitions are not new cases.

Assessment at rounds 0,32,64,96,128 and a normal earlier stopping round uses the
assigned original 392-input M11 panel. Within a pair, its 344 nonordinary inputs
are identical; only the 48 ordinary inputs differ. Between replicates, panels
vary. Fixed-panel assessments are development data used for nomination, not
confirmation. Each population gets exactly 96×96 nominal train queries and
115×392 nominal assessment queries. Nonselectable A0 padding fills unused
assessment slots. Physical genotype/query deduplication is explicit. Equal nominal
budgets do not imply equal CPU. Fresh seeds: search 2026102160, confirmation
2026102161; discarded fixtures 202610210160/+1. All prior and fixture layouts are
excluded, then every generated/rejected/unqueried search layout from confirmation.

## Common selection, variation and imperfect intermediates

Use the exact M10 M1 runtime, C6-focused mutation mixture and selection in both
arms. Every round: 44 survivors and 52 births (seven fully random immigrants,
45 single-opportunity mutations). Half the operator choices change the final C6
writer gate; half use generic M1 mutation with destination forced to C6 after its
normal block draw. No-op proposals are charged. No founder/breeding/survival quota
is introduced. Every protected reference remains immutable.

Both arms rank by zero false no-path, minimum nonempty fixed-parent skill
retention, specialist-only pair coverage, mean nonempty parent retention, then
old domain-balanced correctness/partial scores. All candidate inference is local;
BFS and paths appear only in external generation/grading. Retention is measured
on the entire supplied batch, including fresh ordinary cases, not bank-only.

Survival keeps 12 protected entries, eight merit entries, up to 12 additional
coverage specialists and up to 18 genotype-distinct finite-panel neutral variants;
lexicase fills any remainder to 44. These are capacity bounds, not additive
promises. Archive size 16: four ranked plus complementary coverage. Full-retention
PASS is NOT required for every child, survivor or parent. Imperfect intermediates
and useful specialists can reproduce. This retained capacity is common to both
arms; the experiment does not independently test neutral-reserve policy.

Three fixed nomination roles: integrated, upstream-exploratory and ordinary,
using the existing M10 ranking. Round-zero nominees become frozen references.
No replacement of roles, selection on confirmation or extra candidate panel.

## Prospective outcomes

Fresh confirmation after choices are committed/pushed uses the unchanged
1680-query builder: 400 complete routing pairs,200 corridors,256 ordinary bases
in two poses,168 disconnected inputs. Report queried-only family/size/length
strata, including 16+ routes. Familiar grammar variants and correlated poses are
not a generalization guarantee. Every selected genome, exact root, immediate
parent, frozen/protected reference and two separate intact controls gets the SAME
cases. BFS/A*/bitwave controls are external and included in the final audit.

Individual integration PASS requires a positive M12 mutation depth, zero false
reachable no-path, nonempty fixed-parent success sets in all four classes, 100%
retention of that parent's pairs/corridors/ordinary-both-pose/correct-no-path sets,
and ALL specialist-only pairs (at least eight across two templates). This tests
integration of existing capabilities, not novel routing beyond the protected union.

PRIMARY policy score, integrated role ONLY: specialist-only pair count when the
FULL integration gate passes; otherwise zero. Descriptive bank support requires
positive differences in at least two of three pairs AND a positive summed
difference. Secondary roles cannot rescue this endpoint. No significance claim.

Separately retain the unchanged deeper acquisition gate: at least eight NEW
nonfork prefix2+ complete pairs across two contexts beyond exact root AND all
12 protected references; >=90% own-root complete skills including correct no-path;
>=90% nonempty A0 correct-disconnection set; zero false no-path; positive M12
mutation depth. Long-ordinary/combined gates also remain unchanged. Report losses
and partial specialist gains even when gates fail. Inherited skills, phenotype
neutral edits and multiple aliases are not new acquisitions or independent solvers.
