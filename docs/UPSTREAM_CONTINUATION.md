# M6: longer continuation from acquired upstream-routing specialists

Authorized October 21, 2026 after completed, audited M5. This is a new bounded
phase. It does not extend old allowances, overwrite old histories, or use inspected
confirmation cases for training. M1 remains the runtime; M5 holding did not win.

## Question and starting material

Can sustained generic mutation extend the acquired one-cell-upstream routing
mechanic to second junctions and cycles with prefixes 2/4/8, while retaining
existing routes and correct disconnection detection? More evolutionary time is
an empirical opportunity, not a guarantee of a general solver.

Three populations of 96 use independent future RNG histories and the SAME six
protected M5-informed references, in this fixed order:

1. M5 `a1/standard/0191`: exact neutral-embedding M1 body; new prefix1 upstream skill.
2. M3 A0 `m3/modular/0/mixed/g669/b31`: ordinary/corridor/no-path specialist.
3. M5 `a0/standard/0434`: prefix2 fork specialist, with known retention losses.
4. M3 A1 `m3/modular/2/single/g800/b15`: original prefix0 junction/cycle specialist.
5. A0's exact M3 root `m2/a1/d2/00410`.
6. A1's exact M3 root `m1/modular/1/broad/mutation/g84/b11`.

Each population has these six protected identities plus 30 explicit clonal slots
for EACH of references 1–3, giving 96. No new founder screen or performance-based
choice from old final data is performed. Exact M5 source records and generic
mutation events remain in source_panel.json.gz; neutral writer metadata is checked
and removed. M6 inference uses the unchanged M1 VM. Clones are not mutations or
independent origins. M6 mutation depth starts at zero; inherited M3/M5 ancestry
and M5 mutation opportunities are reported separately. Exact prior roots and
original journals remain preserved. New random immigrants keep this an ongoing
random-origin discovery experiment. No known solver or sexual recombination
enters reproduction. Engineering controls remain outside all populations.

## Selection and reproduction

Same M3 survivor mechanism and merit weighting: 44 survivors include six exact
protected copies, eight merit survivors, up to 12 additional success-coverage
survivors, up to 18 genotype-distinct finite-panel neutral variants, then lexicase
fills slots. Parent selection is epsilon-lexicase on correct/valid/false-no-path/
partial grades. Merit weights paired graph/prefix strata, ordinary family/length
strata, corridors and disconnected families equally. Retaining a protected copy
preserves the population's repertoire; it does NOT give that skill to a nominee.

Each generation adds seven fresh random immigrants and 45 children with ONE
unchanged generic M1 mutation opportunity each. No-op edits remain logged and
charged. Eight persistent channels, at most 256 instructions and 256 ticks,
4096 reads/site and 32768 instructions/site, as in M1. No new operators, output
cleanup, oracle inference or forced backtrace. Archive of 16 uses merit and
correct-input coverage. Fixed initial nominees supply cheap no-evolution references.
This is a discovery run, not an equal-compute causal comparison of search policies.

## Prospective success criteria

Confirmation has 1680 fresh inputs from the M3 builder: 400 complete routing
pairs, 200 corridor inputs, 256 ordinary reachable bases in two poses (512 inputs),
168 disconnected inputs. Report perfect11, perfect15, random9 and random15
separately, with length bins and route lengths. Familiar grammar and correlated
poses/variants limit generalization. Ordinary size15 provides declared size transfer.

Every acquisition gate requires an M6-mutated descendant, zero false reachable
no-path, at least 90% retention of its exact M6 root's successful pair, corridor,
ordinary-both-pose AND correct-no-path sets. Empty root denominators are N/A.
In ADDITION require at least 90% of protected A0's successful disconnected cases,
with a nonempty A0 denominator. This non-vacuous requirement stops a root with
zero disconnection skill from trivially passing retention. It is deliberately
stronger than M5's gate, which remains historically PASS despite its no-path loss.
No new primary claim may hide that loss behind fewer false no-path answers.

Upstream acquisition: at least eight NEW complete non-fork prefix>=2 pairs,
across at least two template contexts, absent from BOTH the exact M6 root and
the protected six-reference union, plus the shared retention/disconnection gate.
Long-ordinary acquisition: at least four NEW both-pose bases with distance>=8,
both perfect and random represented, at least one distance>=16, absent from
root and protected union, plus the same gate. Combined transfer requires BOTH.

Also report all raw new/lost skills, no-path gains/losses, exact-root/parent/frozen
comparisons and exploratory upstream improvements even if a gate fails. A
specialist can be useful without satisfying the combined goal. No general-solver,
multirange, sexual-compatibility or speed claim follows from these finite tests.
