# M4 mechanism and retention assay — complete and audited

October 21, 2026. Registration **305c068** and the complete concrete panel
**c4608da** were pushed before fresh maze generation. No evolution, mating,
adaptive selection, new temporal traces or second corpus was performed.

**The useful result is a causal explanation of two local improvements, together
with a clear retention problem.** Suppressing an interfering channel improved
the retained-skill lineage. Activating a persistent latch created the junction
specialist's successes. Neither the original genomes nor any of the registered
diagnostic edits combined their abilities at the required retention level.
This narrows the next research question to adding context-dependent computation
without disrupting an already useful dynamical system.

## What was fixed and tested

The two original M3 descendants were mixed0 `g669/b31` (A0, retained-skill) and
single2 `g800/b15` (A1, junction specialist). Their exact roots and immediate
parents were included. They remain random-origin descendants: 64 and 73 M3
mutation births respectively. No known solver entered either lineage. A0's
immediate root is M2 `a1/d2/00410`; A1's is the M1 delayed-wave descendant
`modular/1/broad/mutation/g84/b11`. They share earlier ancestry and are not
independent evolutionary replicates.

For each lineage we tested all 256 combinations of its own exact-root and child
blocks, including each block's input ports. We also zeroed each of the eight
child block writers separately, and tested the two components of A0's last
two-edit birth. These are diagnostic edits, not evolved offspring. No blocks
were exchanged between A0 and A1. Eight fixed random references and separate
intact flat/modular engineering controls completed the panel.

All 546 aliases, representing **542 distinct physical genomes**, were published
before the cases existed. Every physical genome received the same 1,680 fresh
inputs: 400 paired routing opportunities, 200 corridor inputs, 256 ordinary
reachable bases in two poses, and 168 disconnected inputs. All 1,362,003 prior
whole layouts were excluded. Cases use the existing grammar and correlated
poses; they do not establish arbitrary topology transfer.

## A0: the last gain comes from suppressing interference

Static dependence finds 14 potentially relevant primitive nodes within the
104-instruction genome. This is a conservative dependency count, not a count
of causally necessary operations or of operations actually executed per tick.

A0's final birth first duplicates a block-4 expression and then swaps two
block-6 input ports. The registered counterfactuals resolve their contributions:

* The first-edit-only intermediate matches the parent on **all 1,680 terminal
  masks, statuses and stopping steps**.
* The second-edit-only counterfactual matches the child on all those outcomes.
* Zeroing the child's channel-6 writer also matches the child on all those
  outcomes. Read/dispatch costs are separate and are not asserted identical.

The port swap changes the relevant channel-6 read from a northern channel-3
value to a northern channel-6 value. Channel 6 begins at zero and has no
independent source in this child computation. Static induction predicts that
it remains zero; the fixed zero-writer intervention agrees with that prediction.
Thus the last improvement is consistent with eliminating an interfering signal,
not discovering an additional propagation mechanism.

Relative to its immediate parent, the child gains 48 complete routing pairs,
24 corridors and nine both-pose ordinary bases without losing any of those
complete successes. It nevertheless loses **17 individual previously correct
inputs** while adding 97, so this is not universal monotonic improvement.

Zeroing any of channels 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 7 destroys all 96 complete pairs and
all 200 corridor successes. Some zeros also create false no-path claims. These
are conditional intervention effects under the fixed execution rules, not
proof that every underlying instruction is necessary.

Only four of the 256 ancestral-block combinations preserve at least 90% of
A0's own paired, corridor and ordinary successes with no false no-path: masks
0, 2, 128 and 130. They differ only in whether blocks 1 and 7 are ancestral.
All keep child blocks **0, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6**. They share the same success counts,
but only mask zero has completely identical terminal masks/statuses/steps.
The wider ordinary ability is therefore sensitive to combinations of evolved
blocks. Restoring ancestral block 6 is harmful even though forcing the child
writer to zero is harmless: replacement and silencing are different interventions.
No A0 restoration acquires a hard pair, and none exceeds 21 ordinary successes.

## A1: a latch creates junction skill, with an unresolved trade-off

This child has 91 instructions and seven potentially relevant primitive nodes.
Its last mutation duplicates an existing SELECT expression into block 0's final
output position. The parent accumulates channel 4 into channel 0; the child
instead keeps a positive old channel-0 value, otherwise setting it from
`channel4 OR goal_flag`. This is a persistent latch. A directional northern
read in block 4 couples it to the wave/clock computation.

The exact parent solves no complete routing pair on this panel. Its one-mutation
child solves 64, including the 32 harder pairs. That places the acquisition at
this recorded last birth on these cases. It also adds four false no-path claims.
No new temporal trace was run, so the detailed intermediate spatial trajectory
is not inferred solely from this terminal result.

Zeroing writers 3, 5 or 6 leaves every terminal mask/status/step unchanged.
Zeroing any of 0, 1, 2, 4 or 7 removes all complete paired successes. Thus much
of the physical genome is outside the effective routing mechanism on this panel.

Restoring only ancestral block 0 recovers **200/200 corridors**, removes all
false no-path claims and retains 48 easy pairs, but loses all 32 hard pairs.
Across the full factorial, all 64 variants retaining the hard pairs keep BOTH
child blocks 0 and 4; all have the same 64-pair, 52-corridor, three-ordinary and
four-false-no-path totals. No combination of the other ancestral blocks removes
that trade-off. This is evidence about this particular block neighborhood, not
a proof that a nearby generic mutation or a different architecture cannot help.

## The prospective combined-retention screen

Both reference eligibility checks passed: A0 adds five 8+edge ordinary successes
beyond its exact root, and A1 adds 32 hard pairs beyond its exact root. The test
was therefore informative under its declared rules.

**Zero diagnostic variants passed** retention of at least 90% of EACH original
child's successful pairs, corridors and ordinary bases with zero false no-path
on reachable inputs. All results, including failures and all 512 restoration
masks, are retained in `summary.json`. The engineering controls solve all 1,680
inputs; none of the eight random references solves a pair, corridor or ordinary
base. One random reference falsely calls every reachable case disconnected.
Those eight draws are sanity checks, not an equal-budget evolutionary control.

This assay did not test inter-lineage crossover, arbitrary new code or a new
regulatory architecture. Its negative screen cannot establish that the skills
are intrinsically incompatible. It does show that straightforward restoration
of existing ancestral blocks does not provide a retention-preserving combination.
There is no selected edited winner to promote or confirm from M4.

910,560 physical calls, 1,051.54 summed candidate seconds, 1,074.91 worker CPU
seconds and 319,210,689,697 charged work units. Sampled process-tree RSS peaked
at 2.633 GiB. There were zero uncertain attempts, guard stops or bypasses.
All registered limits remained intact. Pre-registration software controls used
3,360 additional calls on old excluded fixtures, 0.633 candidate seconds and
0.888 wall seconds; they are not scientific discoveries.

The first registered audit **PASSED in 105.50 seconds**. It regenerated the exact
panel, fresh corpus and task specifications; independently regraded all 910,560
saved outputs; and matched 110,419 native replays. Every model with any
non-factorial alias was fully replayed; factorial-only models used the fixed
1/16 hash sample. The audit verified the canonical summary, complete journal
chain and both intact controls. It shares the native VM with production and is
not a second interpreter. No conventional latency comparison was performed.

Eight focused new/modular tests passed, not a fresh full repository suite.
All 280 registration hashes and dependency versions remain unchanged. All 334
prior M3 completion hashes still verify. No registered scientific source or
output was repaired. A retrospective reporting helper initially included
unqueried metadata in length-stratum denominators; the denominator check caught
it before publication. The corrected helper restricts to the 256 queried
reachable ordinary bases. Initial reporting artifacts and the correction note
are preserved; the registered summary and PASS audit were unaffected.

The original SQLite journal and a verified SQLite-API backup are preserved

## Recommendation

Keep both original lineages, but use A0's retained-skill phenotype as the stronger
reference for the next acquisition study. The evidence favors testing **safe
conditional updates** before spending another long run on unchanged variation.
The question is whether a new computation can activate in the relevant context
while leaving a useful wave and its stored state intact elsewhere.

A concrete next proposal is a small controlled accessibility pilot for generic
conditional state writes: an inactive writer can explicitly *hold its previous
state*, rather than obtaining zero from an unexecuted scratch node. The current
VM can already express holding through SELECT, so this is an evolvability and
mutation-accessibility hypothesis, not a claim that the present machine cannot
represent a solver. An added option must default to the current behavior so
existing genomes are embedded without changing their outputs. It must not supply
distances, parent pointers, route cleanup or a backtrace algorithm.

Register the representation/operator comparison separately, keep evaluation
allocations matched, retain unchanged and random/resampling references, and
require new **upstream** junction/cycle successes alongside existing-skill
retention and zero false no-path. Test on fresh layouts after excluding M4.
Only a passing prospective result would justify a longer search or a later
compatibility/recombination study. M4 itself is closed; no new pilot or continued
evolution has been launched. The general-solver goal remains open, with no
general-solver, multirange, speed or sexual-composition claim from this assay.
