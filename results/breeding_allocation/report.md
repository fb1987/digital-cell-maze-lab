# M7: breeding opportunity changed; the targeted routing barrier remained

M7 is complete, independently confirmed and audited. The registered primary
comparison is a **zero–zero tie in all three replicate pairs**. Reserving births
for four preserved lineages did not produce new nonfork prefix2/4/8 routing pairs.
All18 nominee roles fail the upstream, long-ordinary and combined acquisition
gates. This is evidence against breeding starvation being the sole explanation
for the recent plateau under these conditions. It is not evidence that random-origin
evolution is impossible, or that longer search could never help.

There are useful, bounded results. A single-edit shared-selection descendant
recovered its M6 ancestor's corridor losses while retaining that ancestor's tested
skills. Quota descendants acquired a few short ordinary routes beyond the
protected repertoire, with substantial losses elsewhere. None establishes a
general solver, faster inference, multirange benefit or sexual compatibility.

My recommendation is to preserve the new shared0 repair and the short-route
specialists, but not immediately spend another long run on the same allocation
change. We now know that reserving births can keep lineages active, and that this
alone did not extend their demonstrated routing depth. standard0191's repeated
root sampling also warns that breeding access and sustained inheritance are
different problems; a survival-reservation experiment would be a separate change,
not a justification to reinterpret M7 as a quota success.

The next useful step is a bounded mechanism/retention assay of the single-edit
shared0 repair and quota0's block6 duplication, using exact roots/parents and
prospectively frozen generic perturbations. The question is whether the repairs
create a separable, heritable output-selection mechanism or merely recover
familiar local special cases. Compare on fresh matched longer-prefix/ordinary
challenges, with the current retention/no-path gates. Use those results to choose
between protected lineage niches and a representation/accessibility change before
another long campaign. Keep random-origin discovery open, preserve all histories,
and do not inject a hardcoded route extractor. No next experiment is launched here.
