## Interpretation and proposed next step

M14 changed the fate of behavioural variants without producing a confirmed solver
advance. All six primary nominees emit the same masks/status/steps as the fixed
parent on all1680confirmation inputs:96/400complete pairs,200/200corridors,
15/256ordinary bases correct in both poses,168/168correct disconnections and zero
false no-path. Every nominee remains0/96target nonforkprefix2+pairs and0/77ordinary
16+edge bases. The primary score is0versus0in allthree pairs. All18roles fail
integration/deeper/long/combined gates. No nominated identity solves even one fresh
individual input beyond the protected union. Their inherited longer successes,
including8/9-edge parent routes and up to12-edge specialist routes, are not gains.
These15/256ordinary counts must not be interpreted as a decline from M13's20/256:
the fresh panels differ, and the fixed parent also scores15on this same panel.

The allocation nevertheless made a measurable descriptive difference in training.
Among protected-root newborn mutants counted once at first training exposure,
explore produced4837finite-panel terminal profiles unmatched by any protected
reference;1099survived (22.7%). Retention produced3297;162survived (4.9%). Pairwise
survival rates were22.49/23.15/22.52%explore versus5.51/5.02/4.18%retention. This is
about4.6times the aggregate survival rate, not a statistical significance result
or a matched evaluation of identical mutant pools. Both arms had8505newborn mutant
first-exposure opportunities;31explore and8retention immigrant-root descendants
were separately excluded from protected-root comparisons. No novel profile also
retained every fixed-parent skill on that first training panel in either arm.

There were69explore versus25retention newborn mutants with at least one successful
training input beyond the protected union;25and17respectively survived. These are
individual training inputs, often correlated, not complete paired abilities or
independent confirmation. Selection did not preserve every such candidate. The
registered primary endpoint remains negative; these exploratory diagnostics cannot
rescue it or authorize reselection on the final panel.

Across exploration histories:2243explorer presentations spanning1104distinct IDs,
2268reserved offspring,1142/2207previous explorer presentations survived the next
training selection, and500/2232reserved offspring survived their first subsequent
training selection. Counts repeat identities and panels. Explorers reached up to
11M14mutation births while present in these niches; final live/archive maxima were
6/7/9for exploration and9/10/9for retention. The policy therefore enabled sustained
variation but has not demonstrated deeper genealogies or useful computational
complexity. More surviving variation alone is not evidence of an emerging general
pathfinder.

I recommend a new, prospectively registered60–90minute matched continuation from
the exact six final populations, archives, RNG and lineage registries, preserving
both retention and exploration arms and the fixed confirmation standard. Keep
64-round M14 closed; do not silently resume its journal or change its limits.
Record the new phase's ancestry explicitly and exclude all inspected confirmation
layouts. Freeze its candidates before fresh confirmation. Success must be new
complete routing abilities beyond exact roots and protected references, with the
registered retention/no-path requirements; novelty counts remain secondary.
Declare resource and stopping bounds before launching, and avoid repeated short
restarts if the purpose is to test cumulative evolutionary time. The roughly9.5min
M14 search is not a test of that sustained-time hypothesis.

This recommendation is justified as a test of a remaining hypothesis, not by a
confirmed capability improvement. If sustained continuation also fails to turn
these surviving variants into new reliable routes, revisit the evidence connecting
intermediate-quality niches to route extraction and mutation accessibility before
committing to a larger campaign. Preserve random-origin discovery. Do not inject a
known backtrace into these lineages or launch recombination without a new defined
compatibility/operator experiment. No next phase has been launched.
