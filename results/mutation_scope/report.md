## Interpretation and next decision

This pilot found no acquired confirmed capability beyond the preserved reference
union among its frozen nominees, even at the individual-input level. All six
integrated nominees were generation1, depth1 variants with exactly the fixed
parent's complete successful skillsets:96pairs,200corridors,20ordinary both-pose
bases,168correct no-path,zero false. None retained the specialist-only paired
skills while keeping the parent's full repertoire. Five match the parent's
mask/status/ticks on all1680inputs; broad2 matches1590/1680, with no new successful
case. Four ordinary successes of each primary are8 or12edges, but all are inherited
reference skills. All nominees have0/96target nonforkprefix2+ pairs and0/82ordinary
16+ bases. Prefix0/1 competence remains narrow, not general route extraction.

The two replicate0 upstream nominees, focused g16/b21 and broad g16/b32, descend
one mutation from protected shared_child. They retain the SAME complete root
skills:128pairs/9ordinary, including a13-edge perfect case already solved by that
reference. This is not a new13-edge acquisition. The other upstream nominees are
unchanged gated-specialist founder clones (128pairs/15ordinary). A novel genotype,
nonzero mutation count or a visually longer successful route must not be mistaken
for a new algorithmic ability. The fixed parent solves20/256 ordinary bases on
this panel; previous-panel raw counts do not measure acquisition.

Search did explore beyond those early nominees. Final live/archive lineages reach
focused depths21/7/10 and broad14/10/8;8,640mutation births perarm, with broad edits
distributed across every block. This was not a failed implementation of broader
mutation. It was a failure of these two policies to nominate a confirmed advance
within this short, declared budget. The comparison does not establish that C6
restriction never matters, that64rounds exhaust the broader space, or that
random-origin solver evolution is unviable.

### Exploratory selection diagnosis from already-saved development records

This analysis was added after the registered endpoints; it is descriptive, not a
new primary test. It uses each newborn mutant once at its first train exposure,
rounds1–63 (8,505perarm). Round64 births have no subsequent train exposure and are
excluded. Four broad descendants of unprotected immigrant roots are separately
excluded from reference-root comparisons. No new cases, inference or reselection.

| First-exposure outcome | Focused | Broad |
|---|---:|---:|
| Protected-root descendants | 8,505 | 8,501 |
| Terminal behavior unlike every protected reference | 2,372 | 3,300 |
| Those novel behaviors surviving into next population | 193 (8.1%) | 161 (4.9%) |
| Retain all fixed-parent complete skills on that panel | 3,619 | 2,672 |
| Novel behavior AND all-parent retention | 1 | 0 |
| Any successful individual input beyond protected union | 69 | 45 |
| Those individual-input gainers surviving | 48 | 32 |

A terminal behavior is the finite-panel status+mask signature, not semantic
novelty or a useful solver. Novel failed/flooded outputs count too. These
observations are correlated descendants on training cases, not independent tests
or statistical evidence that broad mutation is worse. Different survival rates
also reflect different produced variants. However, they show that changing other
blocks is accessible, while novelty compatible with the full parent repertoire
is rare in these sampled neighborhoods. Some training-case gainers DID survive;
the system is not literally forbidding every imperfect or novel intermediate.
Current selection already contains coverage and finite-panel neutral reserves.

My next recommendation is a separately registered selection comparison before a
longer unchanged search: keep exact protected references, banks, broad mutation
and equal query allocations, but compare current retention-first ranking with
an explicit bounded exploration allocation for distinct behaviors and useful
partial successes. Preserve strict fresh-confirmation acceptance criteria. The
question is whether a lineage can traverse a temporary skill tradeoff and later
recover/integrate, not whether final standards can be relaxed. Novelty alone
must not reward floods or empty outputs. This proposal requires concrete rules,
fixtures, resource limits and preregistration; no M14 has been launched.

The disk engineering succeeded without modifying historical records. Original
M13 journals and verified SQLite-API backups fit under600MiB; allhistorical
exclusions remain accessible via verified references. About16.42GiB remained
free after preservation, still close to the16GiB floor. Further substantive
search needs additional local storage capacity or a verified prospective space
plan; do not remove old histories or lower the floor. More compute remains an
open option, but neither this storage improvement nor this negative pilot is
evidence that the current fitness landscape will yield a general solver.
