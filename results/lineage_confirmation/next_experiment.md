# After V12L — calibrate heritable exposure without pretending suppression is communication

**Proposal only. Not launched or preregistered.** V12L is closed; do not extend,
rerun, reselect on its inspected confirmation cases, or resume G–K. Preserve every
population/RNG checkpoint, fixed panel, raw response, ancestry record and exception.
Exclude **170,840 whole layouts** through L, including all fixture poses.

L passes the two prespecified conditions for this next calibration. The new K2
lineage retains normal short-prefix fork skills across asymmetric goals and exact
translations, improving over its actual entry ancestor. The older broad Branch
lineage again benefits from external nonlocal-value zeroing, now on four independent
templates spanning all three goal forms, with exact normal pair/corridor retention
on the fixed probe subset. These are different lineages and different findings.
K2 itself has no normal-mask change under zeroing. Neither finding proves useful
nonlocal information, a heritable gate, or general routing.

## 1. Define the smallest reproducible exposure treatment

Start with a genome-owned **three-bit exposure mask for radii 2, 4 and 8**. Local
radius-one values remain available. All-on must reproduce the old runtime exactly;
all-off must reproduce existing nonlocal-value-zeroing exactly, including status,
steps, masks and work counters. Apply the mask only at the two existing nonlocal
read operations (14/15); preserve instruction execution, destination assignment,
read charges, expression gates, caps and emission rules. Do not silently turn
inactive reads into free compute. Include the metadata/branch overhead in memory
and work accounting. Specify and test exact mutation events, serialization, digest,
parent tracking and checkpoint/RNG replay before scientific calls.

This is inherited range suppression, initially constant over an organism's run.
It is **not** new time/context-dependent regulation. Existing per-instruction
register gates already regulate expression; do not rebrand those as a discovery.
A later dynamic exposure selector would be a separate hypothesis needing a matched
control and justification from this calibration.

Use a neutral-metadata arm with the identical three stored bits and identical bit
mutation opportunities, but no effect on exposure. Couple RNG draws and ordinary
instruction mutations across arms. Freeze how often a bit edit replaces an
instruction edit, rather than granting the treatment extra mutation chances.
Keep all-on, fixed-zero and ordinary local controls. An outcome equivalent to
all-off is a suppression result, even if useful. Beneficial multirange computation
requires normal successes that need retained nonlocal information and improve the
net objective relative to these controls.

## 2. Calibrate software, neighborhoods and actual selection activation

Use only NEW calibration layouts, with separate subsequent validation/final seeds.
Freeze the panel and neighborhoods before generating/evaluating them. Include old
broad/narrow Branch and their parents, K2 target/parent/entry, the local pulse-based
exception, preserved longer-prefix variants, fresh controls and the 68-gene draw.
Related parent/child models do not count as independent discovery replicates.

First prove all-on/old and all-off/zeroing parity across real board sizes, graph
families, unreachable inputs, caps and output emission. Known solver controls stay
separate from every random lineage. Then profile a declared, performance-blind
sample of bit states and single-edit instruction neighborhoods. Retain every
sample, including failures. This is calibration/selection data, never holdout.
Require a material difference in evaluated behavior and in the parent/elite/nominee
choices produced by the proposed objective, not merely a differently written rank.
K's largely identical searches show why this check is necessary.

Declare numeric screening thresholds and a fixed call/time cap before evaluating
neighborhoods. Include exact retention of normal pairs/corridors and independently
seeded geometry groups; evaluate generalization on another untouched set after
choices are frozen. Do not use L's inspected prefix-four exceptions as tuning
cases or claim far retention when there are no normal far successes. Use matching
budgets and log charged reads, dispatches, active instructions, memory and elapsed
time. Actual-size profiling must precede any larger commitment.

## 3. Preserve a real fresh-origin route into the experiment

L is not evidence that fresh founders need only an exposure bit. K's fresh runs
never completed a queried training pair, including local and resampling controls.
Create NEW adjacent and two-edge paired elementary tasks, mixed orientations,
corridors and disconnected checks, to calibrate a usable partial-credit gradient.
Check that differences actually change reproduction. No useful solver instruction
may be inserted into a nominally random genome. Preserve founders, immigrants,
archives, neutral bits, RNG and all lineages. More capacity remains a testable
hypothesis, not a presupposed cure.

A revised elementary curriculum must be common across the relevant treatments
and stated explicitly. A later gain cannot be attributed solely to exposure if
curriculum, fitness or runtime also changes. Keep carry/fresh, evolution/resampling,
local/evolved and fixed-zero controls within feasible matched budgets.

## 4. Gate any further evolutionary budget

Only after software parity, a usable fitness gradient and measurable selection
activation should a bounded matched pilot be preregistered. Require independent
normal held-out paired improvement in at least two of three evolutionary
replicates, exact protected-skill retention and a genuine treatment contrast before
considering 60–90 minutes. If effects are confined to the old carried Branch family,
report that scope. If all-off wins, preserve it and say suppression worked. If the
contrast stays inactive or new transfer fails, diagnose the bottleneck before
spending more compute. Random-origin discovery remains central throughout.
