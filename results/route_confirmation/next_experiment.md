# Recommendation after V12F — test suppression of the wrong branch

Status: **proposal only; not launched and not preregistered**. V12F is finished.
Do not rerun it or extend the completed V12E search. Preserve all old genomes,
next populations/RNG, selection archives, raw outputs and trajectories.

V12F's target communicates the goal to every labelled junction, yet completes
zero branching routes or paired exit choices. Its earlier 9/24/25-edge successes
were winding, internally unbranched corridor segments. The original checkpoint
still has a few short successes, including two poses of one cyclic goal pair.
The immediate research issue is useful suppression and retention across goals,
not the longest isolated route or the size of a goal-induced state difference.

A justified next experiment should:

1. Construct new minimal one-junction paired tasks spanning short corridors,
   goals on different exits, all orientations, interior sources and a small
   cyclic stratum. Calibrate them with intact/damaged known controls kept
   completely separate from random-origin lineages. Check that partial scores
   distinguish a correct prefix, wrong-branch suppression and full output.
2. Freeze a matched fork of preserved random-origin populations and RNG. Keep
   capacity, runtime, mutation and protected diversity identical in both arms.
   Preserve corridor, short-route and earlier choice variants by pre-existing
   validation criteria; do not promote a genotype because it did well on V12F
   final cases. Specify exactly one curriculum/pressure contrast after the
   construction/calibration preflight, with actual reproduction diagnostics.
3. Require repeatable both-goal branch suppression and completed pairs on
   validation before extending a bounded pilot. Partial communication alone
   must not trigger a 60–90-minute extension. Include frozen controls and a
   separately specified fresh-origin search if affordable within the fixed
   total budget; do not silently seed a random lineage with a known solver.
4. Freeze final selections before an independently generated challenge set.
   Include plain corridors and branched trees/cyclic grids under the SAME
   generator, so the branching question is not entangled with a distribution
   change. Compare independent bases separately from their D4 poses.

Exclude all **125,008 whole layouts** through V12F, including new software
fixtures and all confirmation poses. V12F is inspected data; no retuning against
its cases. Fix exact counts, seeds, objective, budgets, numerical continuation
criterion and audit coverage in a new protocol before candidate evaluation.
No additional compute has been started by this recommendation.
