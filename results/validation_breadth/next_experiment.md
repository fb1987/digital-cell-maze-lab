# After V12I — isolate distance to branch decisions

**Proposal only; not launched or preregistered.** The V12I fixed-pool comparison
and its main 720-second budget are closed. Do not resume it, G, or H, and do not
turn their inspected final cases into validation.

Broad validation increases carried/local Branch complete pairs from 104 to 212,
with positive raw gains in all three historical replicates, but only one passes
the full gate. Carried/multirange Current passes in one replicate; every cohort
fails the two-of-three requirement. Useful corridor and longer-prefix behaviour
can be lost by a replacement. Both selectors and all underlying genomes should
remain available; do not replace the preserved populations with the final-best
model or silently merge discoveries across original lineages.

A specific retained capacity deserves a controlled follow-up. Narrow's
254-gene carried/multirange Branch nominee i5140 completes all 256 short-prefix
fork pairs, plus 80 two-junction and 80 cycle pairs. Those extra-branch successes
are exactly the ten one-edge-prefix contexts, all eight poses. Total successful
paired routes reach eleven edges, but no selected model completes any pair with
a five-to-eight-edge first-junction prefix. This pattern is an association across
different geometries; it is not yet a causal distance diagnosis.

A bounded next study should:

1. Freeze an explicitly I-informed target set before generating new cases:
   the strong narrow/broad Branch pair, their recorded ancestry controls,
   the narrow model with four-edge-prefix fork successes, the inherited local
   broad model with a four-edge-prefix cycle success, the local all-pose short
   cycle model, fresh-origin selections, the 68-gene random-resampling draw and
   a separate intact known control. State that target choice used I results;
   the next mazes must be new. Do not claim to have assessed the whole pool.
2. Calibrate matched graphs that preserve downstream goals, branch arms,
   alternate routes and clutter while varying source-prefix length. Truncating
   an extended source arm can keep the source an endpoint at every distance.
   Include straight and bent prefixes and independently vary a second junction's
   distance where feasible. Save all poses and controls, including unqueried ones.
3. Compare normal completed outputs first. Separately remove the goal pulse,
   static goal flags, endpoint-aware stopping, and nonlocal values, charging
   ablated reads equally. Measure source and junction responses and a fixed
   trajectory horizon. A forced correct frame is not a normal solution, and
   interventions on failing normal cases cannot establish necessity for success.
4. Fix a modest call/time cap and success/retention gate before launch. Use
   independent contexts and multiple inherited lineages rather than counting
   poses as replications. Preserve failed and partial outputs as well as wins.
5. Only then propose a matched evolutionary change aimed at wrong-branch
   suppression or sustained decision transmission. Preserve random-origin fresh
   and carry populations, resampling, local/evolved controls and useful anchors;
   do not seed a known solver into a random lineage. If the diagnosis does not
   support this direction, revise it before committing another 60–90 minutes.

Broader validation can remain a complementary selection archive in that future
design, with corridor and longer-prefix anchors protected equally in both arms.
Do not tune a new rank against I's inspected final results and present it as an
independent confirmation. Greater capacity/regulation remains a hypothesis;
the unmutated 68-gene draw's 80 fork pairs show why size alone is insufficient.

Exclude **all 144,120 whole layouts** through I, including rejected preflight
fixtures, corrected fixtures, every validation pose and matched final controls.
No further candidate inference is authorized by this proposal document itself.
