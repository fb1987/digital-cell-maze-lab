# V12Y: linkage and donor opportunities on fixed histories

**Immediate gate-writer linkage permits substantially more multigene exchange
than full dependency closure, but it does not clear the frozen design screen.**
The next useful long-search contrast is mutation with and without preservation
of related variation. A long sexual-recombination experiment still needs a new
successful activation pilot.

![Complete fixed-parent comparison](mating_opportunity/opportunities.png)

This completed, audited assay reuses all 18,432 mating attempts from X-R1, including
failures, and adds 2,880 uniformly sampled parent probes across all six prior
policies at five population snapshots. It runs no organism inference and creates
no new maze layouts. These are inspected training histories, not independent
evidence of improved solving.

## What the matched comparison says

Expected probability of exchanging at least two changed instructions, using the
recorded shortlist plus eight uniformly sampled other donors:

| Origin | Common maximum segment | Full dependency closure | Immediate gate linkage | Matched gate sham | Unlinked |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Carry | 16 | 0.25% | 2.47% | 2.68% | 3.01% |
| Carry | 32 | 0.20% | 3.89% | 4.54% | 5.49% |
| Fresh | 16 | 1.27% | 4.24% | 4.29% | 4.81% |
| Fresh | 32 | 1.09% | 6.41% | 6.47% | 7.47% |

These are exact pre-mutation probabilities under the registered virtual selection
rule, including attempts with no usable donor. They are not actual retained
transfers or percentages of mazes solved. Every row pools9,216 opportunities
from three historical policies and three original replicates; those policies
share starting pools and are not nine independent rediscoveries.

All rules see the same parental opportunities and common segment pools. The
lighter rule protects each persistent output's gate and its possible immediate
writers. It does not recursively protect every operand dependency or certify a
functional module. The sham preserves the same number of admissible segments
without following those relationships. Similar gate/sham rates establish no
special functional advantage for gate semantics.

Larger blocks help the lighter rule, but simply adding another length can reduce
full-closure probability: the virtual proposal distributes probability across
the available common lengths. Opportunity counts and actual selection
probabilities are different quantities.

The design screen required 12% expected pre-mutation transfer for gate, gate-sham
and unlinked in two of three replicates of BOTH origins. Neither segment cap
qualifies. This screen is distinct from the future 10% actual post-mutation
activation gate; neither threshold was changed after results.

## Why a broader donor search is insufficient

For 32-instruction gate linkage, the recorded-shortlist view reaches 3.50% carry
and 5.29% fresh; adding eight other donors raises this to 3.89% and 6.41%.
The additional sample helps, but even unlinked exchange remains below the
12% screen. This is a bounded sample, not a proof that no better donor exists.
The alternative views choose a donor uniformly among structurally eligible
shortlisted variants, rather than reproducing the original fitness-complementarity
ranking. The actual historical donor is analyzed separately.

The family snapshots reveal concentration despite almost every genome being
genotypically distinct. Among the three prior sexual policies, effective mutable
family counts fall from 76 to about 2.20 in carry and from 96 to about 3.73 in fresh.
At the final snapshot there are still roughly 94–95 distinct genotypes per 96
members. Small differences around a dominant ancestor do not necessarily provide
multiple differing instructions in one transferable block.

Family counts use fractional ancestry-root membership, excluding protected
identities from the mutable denominator. They measure concentration, not useful
behavior or independent functional innovation. Final snapshots include unevaluated
newborns. The assay does not causally demonstrate that family concentration caused
low solving performance.

## Longer evolution preparation

The user's request for sustained evolutionary time is appropriate to test a
new retention hypothesis. The first prepared 75-minute specification compares
ordinary mutation against mutation with a bounded family-variant reserve,
alongside resampling and frozen controls. It has 24 conditions:
carry/fresh × 3 replicates × 4 policies, 96 genomes each.

Both mutation arms share 44 survivors and 52 births, seven random immigrants,
45 single-edit children,20 protected carried anchors and the same evaluation
schedule. The family arm reserves up to 20 slots for genetically distinct related
variants. The common survivor count differs from X's 24; comparisons must be
within the new experiment. Genetic distance is a retention hypothesis, never
the measure of scientific success.

The [prepared selector](../maze_evolution/family_reservoir.py) and six unit tests
exist. The [long-search setup](../docs/LONG_SEARCH_SETUP.md) separates specifications
from launch readiness: a new runner, exact protocol, fixtures, timing and replay
preflight must be completed before scientific calls. The second 75-minute
specification is a crossover comparison conditional on a fresh activation pass.
No long run is launched by this Y result, and no permission loop is required for
the already authorized implementation/preflight work.

Primary outcomes should be new complete long-prefix pairs, genuine alternative
cycle routing and retention of ordinary/corridor skills on isolated validation
and untouched final cases. Preserve all population/RNG checkpoints and useful
specialists. Do not score more instructions, more families or deeper ancestry as
an enhanced solver.
