## 2. Calibrate a bounded heritable exposure hypothesis

compare a minimal genome-owned nonlocal exposure gate against fixed exposure.
Keep the rest of the runtime, capacity envelope, pressure and case budget common.
Specify exactly which instruction/read is gated, how it is inherited and mutated,
what it costs, and whether inactive reads remain charged. Match storage/mutation
opportunities with a neutral control where needed. Include fixed-zero and local
controls: a gate that merely disables all nonlocal input is a suppression result,
not evidence of useful multirange computation. A beneficial multirange claim needs

a small performance-blind neighborhood sample on carried random-origin models.
