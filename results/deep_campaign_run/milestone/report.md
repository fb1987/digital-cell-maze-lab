# Deep campaign: 10,000-round milestone

**Search complete; full registered selection/mutation/RNG audit PASSED.** The
first audit finished at 21:05:23 UTC on October 20, 2026. All 10,021 transitions
and 1,560,000 births replayed exactly. Capability results remain development data,
not independent confirmation. No confirmation cases have been generated.

The campaign found and retained complementary specialists, but its three final
aggregate primaries are unchanged inherited B genomes. Each solves 104/200
structured goal-switch pairs, all 100 corridor controls, four of 48 perfect
11×11 inputs and six of 48 random 9×9 reachable inputs. Their successful-case
membership matches the historical aggregate reference on the same panel. None
correctly reports any of the 52 disconnected inputs. All population frontiers
remain at prefix two. **A general maze solver and a speed advantage remain
unestablished.** Ten thousand rounds have not produced broad primary improvement.

## What is frozen

The three 96-organism populations completed 10,000 replacement rounds each and
stopped normally on October 20, 2026 at 19:20:12 UTC. The runner selected one
primary and three complementary archive members per island using its registered
rule. Their exact genomes, locus identities and family records, the final
population states and the completion record were pushed in commit `af77416`
before any independent confirmation generation. All 12 selections stay frozen,
including poor ones; this report does not replace them with attractive runners-up.

The original sealed campaign stopped at round 8,268 because its 4×4-room perfect
maze family lacked enough unseen topology orbits. The approved and preregistered
A1 continuation changed only subsequent ordinary-perfect stimuli to 11×11 / 5×5
rooms. The same journal, state/RNG history, selection, counts, runtime and
cumulative limits continued. The 9,000-round and final ordinary totals cannot be
read as changes in ability: panels change, and A1 also changes one domain.

## Actual ancestry and scale

A read-only scan counted **1,560,000 births**: 1,350,000 single-edit mutation births
and 210,000 random immigrants. Of these, 1,289,808 occurred before A1 and 270,192
after it. These counts agree with the fixed reproduction schedule, and the
completed full replay verified every concrete event and population/RNG transition.

The three primaries have zero campaign births in their ancestry because they
were already present at launch. Mutated complements range from one or two
mutation births to **1,858**; the random-immigrant complement has zero. The three island-two complements trace to the
preserved B root `selectivity_v13b/carry/2/current/i238` through 1,858, 1,789 and
1,774 mutation births. Other deep selected lineages include 1,034 and 1,058
mutation births from historical `carry/1/mutation/i18358`, and 977 from B
`selectivity_v13b/carry/1/current/i1175`. The recorded ancestral subgraph contains
3,977 unique campaign events; shared ancestors must not be counted as independent
discoveries. Ancestral mutation births are not a count of useful adaptations or
of distinct functional rule modules.

This was sustained evolution with preserved diversity, not merely a 128-round
pilot. It supplies stronger evidence about this fixed representation and pressure
than the short pilots did, while leaving random-origin discovery open. The
unchanged aggregate winners and limited specialists suggest that further work
should first establish exactly what was acquired and diagnose the remaining
selection/representation bottlenecks. Simply extending this registered phase is
not authorized. No crossover or known-solver injection occurred.
