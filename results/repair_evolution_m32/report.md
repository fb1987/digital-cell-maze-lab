## Reviewed interpretation and next recommendation

The primary null is not merely caused by demanding retention gates. All six fixed INTEGRATED nominees pass the strict gates, yet have zero raw new complete ordinary8+ bases. Each has the same terminal mask/status profile as A0 on all 2416 final inputs: 31 ordinary two-pose bases, 96 paired units, 200 corridors and all 216 disconnected inputs. Their genomes differ, with 1–7 M32 genotype-changing steps, but this finite-panel evidence shows preservation of known behavior, not a new solver mechanism. All 18 fixed-role presentations also have zero raw novel complete ordinary8+ bases. This statement concerns frozen nominees, not every transient mutant ever born.

There is nevertheless real, limited sequential adaptation in the rare specialist background. The source genome itself solves the same 48 controlled final presentations as every REPAIR nominee, including 16 each at 42, 50 and 58 edges. Every REPAIR nominee retains exactly those inputs, gains zero controlled inputs, and still completes **0/48 A/B swaps**. The longer correct paths are inherited ability observed on larger final templates, not an M32 discovery. The earlier source panel's 22/30/38-edge results must not be subtracted from these different cases.

Its individual changes are 37 gains/two losses; its strict ancestral gate still fails and all gains are covered by the fixed portfolio. The allocation treatment actually operated. Each niche population used 496 niche-parent opportunities (eight in each of 62 rounds), with no absent-niche fallback. The sampled number of eligible success profiles was 1–3 in replicate0 and one in replicates1/2. Final rare-entry membership is 32/27/32 in NICHE versus 15/15/16 in SHARED; these counts include protected entries and unchanged clones, not just acquired skills. Niche repair nominees reach 18/20/34 changed steps versus 22/3/1 for shared. Thus immediate lineage extinction or a purely one-step search cannot explain the entire outcome. At the same time, maintaining lineages and accumulating edits did not repair the missing complementary controlled responses. Three related matched histories and secondary descriptive gains do not establish policy causality or significance.

I recommend a separately registered, small mechanism study before spending another long evolution budget: inspect the saved sequential ancestry and outputs around the niche skill gains and persistent A/B failures, then use a fixed set of unchanged entry/parent/descendant genomes for passive traces only if needed. The immediate question is which executed output/termination behavior remains invariant across many edits, and whether the gained corridor/paired skills alter that behavior at all. This can guide a later curriculum, operator or representation hypothesis without inserting a known solution or assuming extra capacity will work. Preserve random-origin diversity and the deeper lineages. M32 is evidence that limited abilities can coexist and improve through serial descent, but it supplies no evidence of new portfolio-level general solving. No M33, new inference study or further evolution has been launched or authorized.
