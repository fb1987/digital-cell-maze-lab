# Proposed V12P: locate and challenge the new cumulative-mutation skill

Status: **proposal only; not launched or preregistered**. V12O is closed. Do not
extend its cap, breed from inspected final cases or turn those cases into
validation. The population-level supply gate failed, so this proposal is a
bounded investigation of a rare confirmed lineage, not a relabeled pass for
large-scale evolution.

## Question and frozen material

The 137-gene local target `mutation_depth/r0/f71/walk0/d16` completes all tested
source-choice pairs with two-/three-edge arms, while its original 128-gene random
founder and depth-1/depth-4 checkpoints complete none. Its intermediate states
are saved. Which transition first produces normal correct output on new cases?
Does that skill survive moving the source away from the decision or extending
the goal arms when downstream geometry is held fixed?

Before generating any scored corpus, freeze a panel containing the founder and
all sixteen consecutive states of this one walk. Include the same physical
programs under local redirection and ordinary nonlocal ranges. Include the two
O depth-16 reachability/corridor descendants, their actual founders and queried
earlier checkpoints, the original M validation anchors, the O unmutated random
adjacent-pair control and a separate intact VM. Preserve all other O genomes,
walks, ineffective edits and RNG states. This panel is explicitly O-informed;
results cannot estimate the population discovery rate.

No new mutation or hand-coded repair enters this first follow-up. All saved
intermediate states are available regardless of the performance of earlier
states; no early performance-based truncation or post-final additions.

## Matched new corpus

Construct fixtures first, then freeze source/protocol and generate entirely new
scientific cases excluding all 178,232 whole layouts through O. Reuse none of O's
final cases as selection data. Use all eight poses in every scored stage.

A compact factorial core should vary source-to-first-choice distance (including
0, 1 and 2 edges) and goal-arm distances (including 2, 3, 4 and a longer stress
level), with straight and turning arms. Choose the exact feasible count from
fixtures and the cost profile before any scientific call. Freeze multiple
independently seeded contexts per geometry; keep their correlation explicit.

The key improvement over O is geometry control. Keep the downstream branches,
extra dead ends and board coordinates fixed while moving the source along an
already present corridor. Where a zero-prefix source has a different degree,
include an explicit degree-matched control rather than silently attributing the
difference to distance. Separately test prefix bends at matched length. Include
matched pruned corridors, disconnected open goals and a small prespecified
stress set with a second junction and genuine alternative-route cycles. These
stress families must remain distinguishable from the primary controlled core.

Validate graph degrees, shared prefixes, both true shortest paths, alternative
cycle lengths and complete pose coverage independently before candidate calls.
Use BFS only for oracle grading, never inside candidate inference.

## Normal outputs before mechanisms

First report normal complete shortest pairs, adjacent/non-adjacent categories,
individual legal versus shortest outputs, corridors, correct no-path and false
no-path claims for every frozen state. Report all consecutive transitions,
including losses. A first observed successful state in this finite panel is not
proof that one isolated edit is sufficient in another genetic background.

Preselect a mechanism subset before scoring: the target, its actual depth-15
parent, the original founder, depth 4 and the intact control. On fresh matched
cases, compare goal-pulse removal, static goal-flag removal, both removed,
endpoint-check removal and nonlocal-value zeroing as separate interventions.
Retain charged read costs. Keep these interventions distinct from the ordinary
local-radius redirection already used in O. Show normal successful cases before
interpreting necessity; changes in failing outputs are not mechanisms of success.

If bounded forced trajectories are registered, label them diagnostic only. They
must not count as normal solutions or justify a posthoc stopping-rule retune.
Any later targeted edit reversion should be a separately frozen experiment with
fresh independent data, not an adaptive repair on the new final corpus.

## Limits and decision rules

Use a fixture-only cost profile and a hard shared candidate cap of at most
12 minutes, including all normal/intervention/final calls. Freeze call, memory,
work and output limits and the complete panel/case counts before the first scored
call. Report profiling, warmup, main inference and exact-replay audit separately.
No extra compute is added because a result looks interesting after inspection.

The primary deliverables are the earliest tested mutation transition, replication
of the source-choice skill across new contexts/poses, and a matched estimate of
prefix/arm-length transfer with retained corridors and no false no-path gains.
Report uncertainty at the seeded-context level and do not treat eight poses as
eight independent discoveries. The lineage was selected in O, so this is
conditional confirmation, not an unbiased comparison of all depths.

A robust new shared-prefix or longer-arm capability would justify designing a
subsequent controlled evolutionary pilot. Persistence only on short choices at
the source would instead motivate a specific runtime/representation hypothesis
or a better bridge curriculum. Either outcome is informative. The failed O
population gate stays failed; a later exploration policy must be justified and
registered explicitly, with equal-budget controls and protected diversity.
Do not automatically start another unchanged 60–90-minute search.
