# Proposed next step after V12W: test linkage during reproduction

Proposal only; not launched or preregistered. W is a fixed diagnostic assay, not
an evolutionary gain. Do not rerun W, extend its closed cap, resume S/T/U/V, or
select/train on W finals. Exclude all251380whole layouts through W, including
unqueried variants and fixtures. Preserve every original population/RNG state,
the longer-prefix specialist, short-junction/cycle anchors and random68 control.
Synthetic W combinations must not silently enter the random-origin populations.

## Decision supported by W

Return to a controlled evolutionary-design test rather than another broad
mutation-dissection series. W localizes the relevant interaction to the original
d07 loci207 and222: the edit12 replacement at207 and the output STORE at222.
Edit12 alone reproduces the destructive STORE effect in every tested background
of edits9–11. With STORE absent, every subset retains124/480positive pairs; with
STORE present, edit12 changes125/480 to0/480. No higher-order interaction involving
9–11 is needed for these correctness endpoints. Edits9/10 still change failed
rasters and work, so they are not universally neutral.

The tested unit is the WHOLE edit12 replacement (gated COPY into18 becomes gated
OR into51), not selective removal of the COPY. Static def–use inspection makes
18→STORE's gate a plausible connection, but W does not isolate that mediator or
prove a complete transferable circuit. This is sufficient motivation for an
operator hypothesis, not a license to encode a hand-built solver.

## One controlled choice: preserve dependencies when crossing genomes

Compare bounded homologous segment crossover with and without a generic rule
that keeps connected scratch-write/output-consumer instructions together.
Do not make a two-gene exception hardcoded to this one lineage. Candidate links
come only from parent instructions: a persistent STORE and its possible reaching
scratch definitions for value/gate, including gated definitions conservatively.
For an aligned locus that is a STORE in either parent, construct the union of
both parents' links, so replacing STORE with EQ cannot evade the linkage rule.
Use bounded dependency closure; a group exceeding the fixed segment/cap limit is
ineligible, not silently truncated or expanded to the whole genome. W's207/222
case is an explicit, W-informed operator fixture; it is not held-out evidence
that this generic rule finds every useful interaction.

Both crossover arms must share the SAME parent/donor selection, homologous
alignment metadata, candidate segment pool, permitted interfaces, size limits,
mutation-after-crossover schedule and proposal cap. The only experimental
constraint is whether a proposed segment may cut a dependency group. Match segment
length opportunities and report both proposed and accepted lengths; include a
size-matched boundary constraint control if exact matching is impossible. Do not
claim that an advantage from simply exchanging larger blocks is a linkage effect.

The old S operator only exchanges differing gaps with equal write/store sets.
Its restrictions often prevent the very STORE/non-STORE allele transfer of
interest here. Any relaxation needed to create the common segment pool must apply
to BOTH crossover arms and be declared as a shared design change. This is a new
operator comparison, not an unchanged repeat of S. Keep ordinary single-edit
mutation as a separate reference; it is required to judge practical usefulness.

Fresh random-origin populations remain an equal primary track. Their dependency
groups must be derived from their own programs; no d07/d12 genes or wiring are
injected. Related descendants can provide homologous variation after mutation;
record actual mating opportunities and activation by origin instead of assuming
fresh unrelated founders can meaningfully exchange code. Use ancestry/immutable
gene identities where available; exact matching anchors alone do not certify
homology or semantic compatibility. A lack of mating opportunities is a result.

## Controlled evolution and gate

Use carry and wholly fresh origins, three independently seeded replicates per
origin, matched initial pools/RNG and fixed96-organism capacity. The four search
policies are dependency-preserving crossover, common-pool unlinked crossover,
single-edit mutation and random resampling; retain frozen initial controls and
common protected capability anchors. All search arms use the same fitness,
selection/archive rules, immigration rate, runtime, per-genome limits and complete
evaluation schedule. Count proposal/rejection cost and fallback mutation, not just
successful matings. Keep multi-goal donor selection fixed across both crossover
arms; paired correctness and complementary training skills can influence it.

Preserve all descendant ancestry, actual transferred instructions, gene identity
mapping, population/RNG checkpoints and raw output masks. A child inheriting one
instruction, an unchanged genotype, or a mutation-only winner must not be counted
as evidence of combining abilities. Report related carried lineages separately
from independent fresh discovery.

Calibrate first on software fixtures and a small new training pilot under a
12-minute cap. Freeze explicit activation thresholds before that pilot: proposed
starting gates are at least10% of scheduled mating births producing an actual
multi-instruction genotype change and at least2/3 search replicates per origin
showing nontrivial accepted matings. The two origins are judged separately; a
carry-only activation result does not authorize a claim about fresh discovery.
If the generic grouping is too conservative to activate, stop at that failed
operator-feasibility result rather than spending75minutes on mutation fallbacks.
This gate checks whether the operator is exercised, not whether it improves solving.

After activation and timing feasibility, preregister one bounded60–75-minute
comparison with equal candidate evaluation counts and a shared wall/compute cap.
Use new training/validation/final layouts; freeze all choices before final. Keep
prefix distance, bends, second junctions and real alternative routes in the task
mix, plus ordinary and disconnected transfer. Prefer independent contexts; restore
all8poses in the eventual confirmation if calibration permits.

Proposed efficacy gate to finalize BEFORE new validation: within at least2/3
replicates of an origin, linked crossover exceeds unlinked, mutation, resampling
and frozen controls on complete prefix≥4pairs by at least16pairs, spans at least
two independent contexts, includes genuine alternative-cycle successes and
retains at least95% of the relevant baseline's paired/corridor/ordinary successes,
without increased false no-path claims. Require actual multi-instruction transfer
in successful ancestry; report its contribution separately from later mutations.
Final confirmation must meet the same frozen gate on new contexts. Archive
specialists equally across arms; aggregate gains cannot erase lost capabilities.

This is a falsifiable proposal, not a guarantee that linkage helps. Protecting a
short-route interaction can prevent damage while still preventing innovation.
General solving, multi-distance benefit and speedup remain unestablished. The
objective is new transferable ability beyond preserved short-prefix behaviour,
not merely retaining the124 W pairs or producing more elaborate genomes.
