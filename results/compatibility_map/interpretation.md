## What the experiment isolates

The original207 instruction is a gated COPY from scratch25 into scratch18. The
original222 STORE writes scratch58 to output channel2, gated by scratch18. Edit12
replaces that COPY with a gated OR into scratch51. The experiment therefore
identifies the **whole replacement**, conditional on the STORE. The apparent
18-to-output-gate connection is a static mechanistic interpretation, not a new
intervention proving whether removal of the COPY, addition of the OR, or their
combination mediates the effect. It is not a complete proven circuit diagram.

Gene identities stay fixed when edit10 deletes original106; later207/222/234
become physical206/221/233. Independent reconstruction follows the historical

## Research decision

The compatibility question is resolved far enough to define a new evolutionary
test. I recommend **bounded crossover that preserves parent-derived regulatory
and output dependencies**, compared with otherwise matched unlinked crossover,
single-edit mutation, resampling and frozen controls. Both crossover arms must
share segment/interface rules and size opportunities; protecting a group must
not be confounded with simply exchanging larger blocks. The old S interface
checks often forbid STORE/non-STORE transfer, so any relaxation must be shared
and explicitly declared. Fresh populations derive groups from their own random
programs; no W circuit is inserted.

The [concrete proposal](compatibility_map/next_experiment.md) specifies activation,
equal-budget, ancestry and efficacy checks. A12-minute feasibility stage should
verify actual multi-instruction transfer before a bounded60–75-minute comparison.
Successful retention of this old short-prefix ability alone is insufficient:
the test must improve independently confirmed longer-prefix routing while
preserving useful diversity. The operator test is **not launched**. W supplies
a testable dependency, not evidence that sexual recombination will now work.
