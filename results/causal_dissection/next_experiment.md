# Proposed next step after V12V

Proposal only, not launched or preregistered. V is closed after its full audit and
seal; do not repeat it, extend its cap, resume S/T/U, repair/reselect primaries on
V finals, or breed the synthetic probes into a supposedly untouched random lineage.
Exclude all243540whole layouts through V, including unqueried cases and fixtures.

V establishes two useful facts on new layouts. The destination mutation works
without the first deletion, and channel4 suppression/restoration exactly matches
its predicted native counterpart in all18944diagnostic responses. But the output
store helps in d07 (+24positive pairs) and harms in d12 (−312). Restoring it to
d12 is NOT a repair. This background dependency is now the concrete obstacle to
rule transplantation or naive recombination.

Proposed bounded compatibility assay:

1. Freeze exact d07/d08/d12 and the four intervening historical edits9–12. Build
   all16combinations of those four edits, each with the original gated output
   store or the charged self-COPY used in V:32genetic conditions before exact-genotype
   deduplication. Keep exact d08/d12 EQ programs as additional controls; do not assume
   EQ remains inert in every new combination.
   Retain original gene identities when applying subsets so deletion-induced index
   shifts cannot change which instruction is edited. Keep every program within
   the existing256-gene cap. Synthetic combinations are mechanistic probes, not
   new evolutionary discoveries. No solver logic or oracle-derived gene is added.
2. Use a new matched corpus and normal inference to map which edit(s) make the
   store harmful, including higher-order interactions and lost abilities. Keep
   ordinary, corridor, short/long-prefix, genuine-cycle and disconnected controls.
   Preserve fresh2/root, carry1/broadroot, random68 and intact as comparators.
   Calibrate first under a fixed12-minute cap. Prefer more independent contexts
   over correlated repeats; freeze any timing-only sizing rule before final data.
3. Turn the resulting dependency map into one testable evolutionary-design choice.
   For example, compare mutation or crossover that preserves a demonstrated
   interacting group against the existing operator, with equal budgets and fresh,
   carry, resampling and no-evolution controls. Do not assume a whole program is
   a transferable module, that extra genes help, or that shielding a known circuit
   automatically improves long-prefix solving. Keep random-origin discovery central.

The factorial assay should be a single bounded way to resolve this identified
interaction, not an open-ended series of post-hoc reversions on the same cases.
Only propose/launch the evolutionary operator comparison after its module and
failure criterion are concrete. Larger compute and sexual recombination remain
hypotheses requiring controlled evidence. All prior populations, useful anchors,
RNG states and raw results remain preserved.
