## Interpretation and next recommendation

zero, so all three paired differences are zero. All18roles fail the registered
integration,deeper-routing,long-ordinary and combined-transfer gates. The sampled
long/deeper limitation remains stark: each nominee solves0/96nonforkprefix2+
complete pairs and0/84ordinary bases with16+edges in both poses. We have not
produced a general solver or established that this search will eventually do so.

parent solves20ordinary bases in both poses. The three bank primaries retain
20/20each. Current primaries retain20/20,5/20and16/20. Their TOTAL ordinary counts
are20,8and18: the latter totals include3and2different successes,which do not

ordinary losses. It retains all32specialist-only pairs,all96parent pairs,all200
corridors and168correct disconnections,withzero false no-path; the ordinary loss

founder,not a new M12 acquisition. Do not call these three histories independent

toward the missing route-selection mechanism.

also C6-focused. That is a material restriction when the eventual goal may require
coordinated exploration,memory and path-output changes. M12 therefore does not
justify closing random-origin discovery,or merely scaling this exact operator
into a much longer campaign.

A next prospectively registered comparison could keep the banks,founders,runtime,

does not settle that combined setup. Preserve a separate requirement for NEW

not an authorized or launched M13experiment,and it is not a guarantee of progress.
