# After V12M — test goal-conditioned branch exclusion in fresh-origin evolution

**Proposal only. Not launched or preregistered.** M is complete/closed after its
scheduled audit; do not rerun/extend it, resume older runs, train on its final
cases or relax its failed carried gate. Preserve all M founders, genotype states,
neutral/zero/local controls, mutation/RNG records and previous populations.
Exclude **175,592 whole layouts** through M, including unqueried poses/locations.

## What justifies a next step

The new three-bit runtime passes old all-on/all-off parity, mutation replay and
accounting checks. Exposure affects selection in the old Branch family and
transfers a suppression benefit, but the final gain spans only straight-goal
templates and equals all-zero exposure. The carried breadth gate fails. Do not
spend another large budget on static exposure or claim retained nonzero bits prove
useful communication. Preserve the variants for later controls.

Fresh numerical gradient screening passes3/3: partial credit changes parent draws
and elites, and selected founders improve the registered final partial metric.
Yet all three neutral selections fill the source-connected component on every
paired input, with identical masks for both goals. This is a transparent limitation
of that screen, not a reason to rewrite its recorded pass. Component-growth and
corridor skills are worth preserving, but their score must not stand in for choosing
a route. M does not test longer evolution or increased capacity, so those hypotheses
remain open. The next step should improve pressure before increasing compute.

## One controlled pressure change, with a preflight that can reject it

Design a paired, goal-conditioned partial-credit component that rewards correctly
excluding the alternative arm at the decision junction while preserving progress
from source and goal. Evaluate only ordinary emitted masks. A response difference
confined to goal-marker pixels is insufficient; changing to the correct downstream
arm must matter. Goal-switch labels and BFS distances remain evaluator-only and
must never enter candidate inference. Keep hard valid/shortest pair completion as
the primary outcome. State exactly where the new partial field enters parent,
elite and nomination selection; the current rank's corridor counts can dominate
partial progress when every paired count is zero.

Freeze the formula and numeric activation/retention thresholds before NEW preflight
cases. Use paired adjacent/two-/three-edge tasks with shared-prefix forks, balanced
poses, matched pruned corridors, isolated clutter and explicit disconnected goals.
Confirm independently that the proposed score separates identical component floods,
endpoint-only masks, partial correct-arm exclusion and complete shortest pairs.
Known/synthetic correctness examples may be used to test scoring, never inserted
into a random-origin population. Save every generated layout, including rejected
software fixtures when candidate calls occurred.

Use the saved M founder pools as explicitly M-informed starting populations and a
performance-blind, bounded sample of their single-edit neighborhoods. No selection
from M final cases. Include the original random populations, the three
calibration/validation-selected component growers, and the validation-selected
local founder81 with equal protected-anchor rules in matched arms. Founder81 is an
unmutated96-gene random draw, not an evolved discovery; its independent final has
16adjacent pairs in four poses of four templates and no complete two-edge pair.
Preserve this useful diversity without treating it as a general solver seed.

Require that the new score changes actual reproductive/elite/nominee choices and
favors a measurable ordinary-output choice improvement over the flood behavior.
Do not pass solely because many random candidates receive distinct numbers or
because a proxy's aggregate mean rises. Check whether single-edit variation can
change decision behavior at all. If the preflight fails, report that clearly and
revise a new prospective proposal rather than launching an unchanged long search.

## A bounded matched pilot only after that preflight

Compare current versus goal-discriminating pressure using identical starting
programs/RNG, capacity, mutation, training schedule and common archive rules.
Include no-evolution/resampling controls and local versus ordinary nonlocal
runtime controls as feasible within the fixed call budget. Freeze exposure bits
or otherwise keep their effect identical between pressure arms; changing exposure
and pressure together would confound the contrast. No known-solver injection.
Any new curriculum is common to compared arms and recorded explicitly.

Use independent replicate RNGs, save every next population/RNG checkpoint and
all useful variants, and freeze validation selections before an untouched final
set. Start with a bounded approximately12-minute pilot, after actual-size/cost
profiling. Preserve incomplete checkpoints if the cap stops it; no retroactive
extension. Report complete paired routes, each side separately, wrong-arm/branch
errors, corridor retention, no-path behavior, work and memory. Variants and poses
are correlated; independent base contexts and independent evolutionary replicates
must remain distinct units.

Require normal held-out non-adjacent paired improvement in at least two of three
independent evolutionary replicates, exact protected-skill retention and a visible
pressure contrast before60–90minutes. Partial-only gains are insufficient for that
larger-run gate. If new evolution produces only component flooding, preserve the
lineages and diagnose the missing decision mechanism rather than calling it a
solver. Random-origin discovery remains the central track throughout.
