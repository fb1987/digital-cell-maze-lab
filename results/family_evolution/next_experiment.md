# Proposal after V12Z

This is a proposal, not a registration or launched experiment. Do not extend the
closed Z cap, resume it silently, or train/reselect on its inspected final cases.
Random-origin discovery remains central. Preserve every population, RNG state,
archive, ancestry record and useful variant.

The long comparison established that the reserve can increase inherited-locus
variation without improving final primary routing. Ordinary mutation found the
junction-start phenotype earlier on validation, but final random resampling
matched it. The strongest new diagnostic is specific: all six ordinary-mutation
and resampling primaries emit the correct route plus exactly one extra cell on
all 320 one-cell-prefix fork inputs/model. At greater prefixes they contain the
route with more extra cells. These remain invalid outputs; no cleanup was used.

## First priority: distinguish stopping from persistent overgrowth

Freeze an explicitly Z-informed panel before generating new scientific cases:
the exact fresh mutation/resampling primaries, relevant family selections,
exact saved roots/parents, complementary historical specialists and a separately
labelled intact control. Root/parent recovery must reproduce stored identities,
not construct a hand-designed solver. Any later gene probes must be labelled
constructed diagnostics and kept outside random-origin populations.

Use entirely new matched contexts that move the source while holding cells,
goals and downstream geometry fixed. Include bends, a second junction, genuine
alternative cycles, corridors and nonisolated disconnected controls. Exclude all
272,464 whole layouts through Z plus every later software fixture. Freeze exact
panel, sample sizes, intervention rules and contrasts after software/timing
preflight, before scientific calls. A bounded local pilot of roughly 12 candidate
minutes is the planning allowance; no automatic extension.

Evaluate normal outputs first. Fixed-horizon diagnostic traces can then test
whether extra cells disappear after native stopping or persist under the same
reaction rules. Separate pulse, static flag and endpoint-stop effects rather
than changing them together. A later correct frame is not a normal solution;
never select the best tick using the oracle. Any proposed stopping improvement
requires its own precommitted inference rule and untouched evaluation.

Record legal paths, shortest paths, complete two-goal pairs, missing/extra cells,
work and native timing separately. Intervention effects on failing trajectories
alone do not establish a mechanism necessary for successful routing. A result
that branch overgrowth persists would support changing the evolvable output
process or its pressure; it would not justify an oracle pruning decoder.

## Recombination remains a separate gated track

The Z family populations now have more structural variation in retained
relatives. Their functional complementarity and ability to transmit multiple
changed instructions remain unestablished. A static opportunity check on these
frozen populations can inform a new pilot; it cannot replace actual activation.
Clearly label any Z-derived initial pool as continued random-origin ancestry,
not a newly sampled fresh population.

Before the prepared 75-minute sexual comparison, a NEW preregistered pilot must
show at least 10% of scheduled mating attempts retaining two changed donor
instructions after mutation, in at least eight generations and two of three
replicates for every sexual policy in BOTH origins. Keep matched unlinked,
linked/sham and mutation/resampling controls and a common donor opportunity set.
Record parental goal complementarity, actual inheritance, offspring correctness
and losses separately. Multiple transferred alleles are not proof of combined
solving abilities. No longer crossover run has been launched or registered.
