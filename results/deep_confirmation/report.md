## Research implication

The long campaign did acquire specific routing behaviors that survive independent
layout sampling, but those behaviors remain narrow and do not accumulate into a
broad primary solver. This supports the next generic representation/curriculum
comparison. The M1 prototype supplies separate expression blocks, private scratch
registers and explicit channel ports; it must earn its place through controlled
random-origin experiments. No distance wave or backtrace is supplied to it.
