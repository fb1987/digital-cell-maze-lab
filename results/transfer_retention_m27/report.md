# M27 — Heritable rare wins, limited transfer across maze orientations

**The original wins are real and reproducible, but the hoped-for complete longer-route
transfer did not appear.** All 30 rare-win identities reproduce their original
both-orientation success, including the 16-edge perfect-maze specialist. One of
that specialist's two mutated offspring retains the exact 16-edge win. Across the
35 recorded offspring of changed rare-win mutants, 15 retain their parent's original
witness; including the random immigrant's offspring gives 17/39.

On fresh cases, **0/28 primary variants add a complete ordinary8+ base beyond even
the 14 protected references**. All also score zero against the larger frozen
historical portfolio, before applying retention gates. None solves an ordinary
route of 16+ edges in both orientations on the 174 fresh bases in that length range.

There is narrower transfer: 18 of the changed variants contribute novel correct
single-orientation responses beyond the historical portfolio, covering 11 individual
inputs from seven bases, with routes of 8–10 edges. On four perfect-maze bases,
different variants solve complementary orientations, but no single tested variant
combines them. That is a finite-panel observation about complementary specialists,
not an evolved combined algorithm or an oracle-free ensemble.

## What was frozen and tested

The user authorized the transfer-and-retention assay after M26. Registration
**dfe415c**, with 380 source/preparation hashes, was pushed BEFORE generating the
scientific fresh panel. The exact pool was frozen at 208 identities and 191 physical
models: all 30 rare-win identities (28 changed mutants, one no-op and one random
immigrant), their 22 distinct actual parents, all 39 offspring born after the rare
wins, roots and initial live ancestors, 14 protected references, all 23 M25 frozen
choice items, all 100 historical archive aliases and three intact correctness
controls. Identity roles can overlap. No genome was edited, no nomination or
selection occurred, and no evolutionary history was resumed.

The primary cohort remained all 28 changed mutants. The no-op and immigrant were
secondary throughout. A 121-record ancestry closure preserves the exact source
bodies and mutation records. These are selected related lineages, not independent
origins or newly randomized evolutionary replicates.

Every physical model received the identical panels:

- **22 reused witness inputs:** 11 saved bases, poses 0 and 4. These are diagnostic
  development data. New runs on these old cases are not independent confirmation.
- **2,128 fresh inputs:** the existing 1,680-input standard panel plus 448 targeted
  inputs, comprising 224 extra bases in both poses. Targeted strata cover perfect
  and cyclic/random mazes, sizes 11 and 15, lengths 8–11, 12–15 and 16–23; size15
  also includes lengths 24–31. There are 16 bases per declared stratum.

The fresh panel contains 480 reachable ordinary bases, including 432 with routes
8+ and 174 with routes16+, plus structured pairs, corridors and 168 disconnected
inputs. Targeted random source components were verified to contain cycles. Standard
random inputs retain the existing generator's distribution. Perfect-maze successes
are tree cases; they do not establish shortest-path behavior on cyclic mazes.

Fresh seed was 2026102301. All prior whole layouts and all discarded preflight
proposals were excluded, including transformation orbits. Length/cycle rejection
uses environment metadata alone, never candidate performance. Two reflected poses
are correlated tests of the same maze, not two independent maze samples.

## Orientation-specific transfer and complementarity

Eighteen changed mutants add novel individual ordinary8+ inputs beyond the full
historical portfolio. Their 55 identity/input presentations collapse to 11 unique
inputs on seven bases. Fifty-two presentations are on perfect mazes and three on
random mazes; repeated variants and cases are not independent evidence. The novel
route lengths are8,9 and10 edges.

For each novel successful orientation, the same variant fails the other pose:
29 counterpart presentations emit disconnected output,14 emit a branch/cycle, and12
exhaust the read budget. The successful poses split27 versus28 between poses0/4.
This is not simply every organism preferring the same global orientation. Terminal
rasters identify failure categories; they do not prove a causal internal mechanism.

Four perfect-maze bases have a novel successful pose0 response in one variant and
a novel successful pose4 response in another. This preserves useful material for
a composition hypothesis, including the user's earlier interest in combining
specialists. No recombination, regulated mixture or ensemble was built or tested
here, and an oracle choosing the successful genome for each case would violate the
single-organism goal. A future combination must itself decide correctly from the
maze through a bounded inherited mechanism.

## Research interpretation and next recommendation

M26's rare wins were not logging artifacts. Their exact reproduction, partial
inheritance and limited new single-orientation performance support heritable local
heuristics. M27 substantially narrows the stronger interpretation: it supplies no
confirmed new complete ordinary8+ capability beyond the existing reference pool,
and no robust16+ transfer in the primary cohort.

Current evidence points toward consistent routing and skill composition as the next
obstacles to test. More compute alone is not yet justified as the next intervention
for this unchanged setup. This fixed assay does not decide whether longer histories,
a richer regulated representation or improved variation can eventually succeed;
the random-origin track and its checkpoints remain valuable and preserved.

**Next proposal: a small, separately registered integration-accessibility assay.**
Use the now-observed cases explicitly as development data to investigate whether
complementary specialists can be combined within one organism while preserving
source/goal connectivity and disconnection handling. Generic regulated composition
or compatible genetic recombination could be compared with matched ordinary mutation,
with all variation opportunities and costs charged. Recombination is a hypothesis
about accessibility, not an assumed merger of abilities. It should first demonstrate
bounded inference, preserved old behavior where intended and useful combined outputs.

Freeze all resulting choices before a separate untouched confirmation panel. That
would test an explicit mechanism for accumulating skills before committing another
large search allocation. It must not insert a known solver, use per-case oracle
selection, rebrand M27's used cases as new confirmation, or discard the original
random-origin histories. No next assay, evolution, recombination, capacity change,
solver injection or cloud compute has been launched or is authorized by M27.
