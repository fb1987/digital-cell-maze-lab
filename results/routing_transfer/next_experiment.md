# Recommendation after V12H — test selection coverage before more breeding

Status: **proposal only; not launched or preregistered**. V12H is a fixed-genome
confirmation; its inference budget is closed. V12G's populations/RNG and all prior
source/results remain intact. Do not resume either closed experiment.

The result narrows the problem. The prior G leader retains 690/768 matched
corridors but completes no branched H outputs. An older local paired variant,
already present in the G frozen controls, completes 17 one-junction, 15
two-junction and three real-cycle paired poses. All are short, and no base works
in all eight poses. The cycle result is one six/six-edge context with 18/18-edge
alternatives. Every model fails the long paired-transfer screen. There is no
replicated new evolutionary advantage or speedup.

This does not isolate source-prefix length as the cause of G-to-H failure. H also
changes board size, geometry and sampling. Its source-to-goal junction prefixes
are 4–8 edges; G used at most three. The matched H graphs identify sensitivity to
extra branches within H, but a between-study numerical comparison is not a
single-variable causal test. The four prespecified mechanism targets all fail H
normal pairs, so their ablations cannot establish which inputs were necessary for
their earlier G successes. Their measured goal responses remain useful evidence,
distinct from correct routing. The successful inherited local H variant was not
in the mechanism subset; no post-test reselection/probing was added.

The next bounded experiment should implement the deferred **validation-breadth
comparison on a frozen existing genome pool**, with no breeding initially:

1. Freeze all G validation-nominated genotypes by a recorded, performance-blind
   membership rule, preserving topology/replicate/origin and random-resampling
   controls. Do not use H scores to choose a subset or transfer genomes between
   lineages. Deduplicate physical evaluation while retaining every alias and
   archive; any immutable common anchors must be equally available.
2. Construct wholly new validation cases spanning G-like and H-like source
   prefixes, short and longer goal arms, two-junction trees and real alternative
   cycles. Include matched corridors. Vary source-prefix and goal-tail distance
   explicitly, and balance placements/poses. Do not change selection after
   viewing the new final corpus.
3. Compare concentrated versus broad validation with **the same number of
   input evaluations per genome**. One viable starting design is four contexts
   × eight poses versus 32 contexts × one balanced pose, each with two goals:
   64 inputs per genome. Match stratum proportions and any corridor allowance.
   This compares coverage with repeated symmetry testing, not simply granting
   one selector eight times the computation. Multiple fixed validation draws
   must be shared across conditions; choose exact counts/caps before launching.
4. Freeze both selections, then evaluate a separate untouched corpus. Primary
   evidence should be completed pairs across independent contexts and replicate
   consistency, with individual shortest routes, corridor retention, no-path
   handling, inference cost and inherited-versus-fresh contrasts reported too.
   Define a numerical gain/retention gate before evaluation.

Keep the initial study modest (roughly a 10–12-minute candidate-call cap after
profiling and calibration, with fixed maximum calls). If broader coverage
improves transfer across replicates, use it in a subsequent matched evolutionary
run from preserved populations/RNG, with fresh random origin, resampling, local
and evolved ranges retained. Only then decide whether 60–90 minutes is justified.
If broader selection also fails, the next hypothesis should address the mapping
from goal information to branch suppression, not merely more repeated exposure
to the same tiny grammar.

Random-origin discovery remains central. The correct small cycle outputs are
worth preserving; the negative transfer result does not show that discovery is
impossible. The intact control also solves the whole corpus in the existing VM,
so lack of representational ability at the current hard capacity has not been
demonstrated. Greater complexity and regulation remain testable hypotheses, not
an explanation established by these results.

Exclude **all 138,712 whole layouts** through H, including every fixture, pose,
pruned corridor and cut control. Keep H final cases as inspected test evidence;
never silently relabel them as validation or fresh holdouts. Any future diagnostic
on G/H successful cases must be explicitly identified as reused-case mechanism
work, not independent confirmation, and separately bounded.
