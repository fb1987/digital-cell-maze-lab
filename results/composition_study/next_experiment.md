# Proposed mechanism and transfer comparison after V12S

This is a proposal, not a preregistration or launched experiment. V12S is closed;
do not resume its search, extend its allowance, or select/train on its final data.

The useful new evidence is sustained-search acquisition of partial routing, with
clear failures as junction distance increases. The strongest recombination-arm
primary has mutation-only ancestry. Most actual exchanges transfer one instruction,
so V12S does not establish that substantial functional modules can be combined.

The next question should be whether the new abilities implement selective routing
or a limited wave/stopping shortcut. Before another large search, freeze a small,
explicitly S-informed panel containing:

- Fresh mutation replicate 0 primary `fresh/0/mutation/i13694` and its exact
  random immigrant root `fresh/0/mutation/i288`.
- Fresh mutation replicate 2 primary `fresh/2/mutation/i18042` and its exact
  random immigrant root `fresh/2/mutation/i2958`.
- Carried mutation replicate 0 primary `carry/0/mutation/i3776`, its preserved
  narrow-Branch root, and the longer-prefix carried mutation replicate 1 primary
  `carry/1/mutation/i18358` with its broad-Branch root.
- If the budget permits a separate recombination question, the actually spliced
  `carry/0/recombination/i20652` and its three historical panel roots. Its ancestry
  also contains ordinary mutations, so parentage alone cannot demonstrate composition.

Use a completely new matched corpus, excluding every whole layout through S,
including all fixtures and constructed unqueried poses. Vary source-to-junction
distance, bends, asymmetric goal arms and second-junction placement independently
where possible. Include unfamiliar route forms, true alternative cycles and
disconnected components whose goals are not isolated. Keep both goal tasks and all
poses paired. Freeze the panel, corpus grammar, metrics and stopping rules before
generation; no final-based replacement.

Normal complete shortest outputs come first. Compare roots and descendants on the
same cases. Where a normal success exists, use predeclared, separate interventions
on the goal pulse, static flags, output channel and stopping inputs to distinguish
propagation, selective output and halt timing. Forced trajectories remain diagnostic
and cannot be relabeled successful inference. Use saved mutation paths to identify
candidate transitions without inserting a routing algorithm or decoder.

The S saved-output observer provides a specific hypothesis: a goal-distance ball
followed by one pruning round matches2,016/2,304branched rasters for fresh mutation
replicate2; two rounds match2,016 for the actual spliced carry0 selection; three
match2,048 for carry0 mutation. These fits include wrong outputs and are post-hoc,
not causal demonstrations. The future test should distinguish finite pruning,
signal propagation and endpoint stopping; it must not silently replace a genome
with this oracle-based explanatory predictor. Other variants fit the family less
well, so do not assume one universal mechanism.

Preserve the prefix-four specialist even if its aggregate count is lower. A useful
advance must retain complementary abilities or explicitly report their loss.
Treat context/base geometry as the independent unit; pose copies and new clutter
do not supply independent evidence of arbitrary structural generalization.

No further 60–90-minute run is justified solely by S's aggregate gains. A subsequent
evolutionary change should address an identified mechanism or transferable interface.
Recombination remains an option: stronger inherited module boundaries or semantic
interfaces could make larger transfers meaningful, but adding them changes the
representation and needs its own controlled comparison. Random-origin populations
and all original ancestry/RNG checkpoints remain central and preserved.
