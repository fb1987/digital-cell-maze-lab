# Proposed V12Q: evolve path isolation across the first internal junction

Status: **proposal only; not launched or preregistered**. P is complete. Do not
repeat it, extend its cap, reuse inspected final layouts as validation, or
reinterpret O's failed population-supply gate as a pass.

## Hypothesis and scope

P localizes a random-origin gain at saved edit9 and shows that the emitted raster
matches a goal-centered distance ball. This can solve a single arm ending at the
source, but spills into wrong branches when the source lies beyond a junction.
The next question is whether evolutionary pressure on that small, precisely
identified gap can produce route isolation while retaining the useful spreading
behavior. This is a conditional evolvability test, not a claim of replicated
from-scratch discovery or an automatic justification for a long run.

Use a bounded pilot, at most twelve candidate minutes after fixture profiling,
with unchanged runtime, instruction set, goal inputs, 256-gene ceiling, stopping
rules and single-edit mutation operator. Start with local topology because the
identified feedback depends on nearest neighbors. Preserve all nonlocal genomes
and results; this pilot does not test or dismiss future multirange potential.
No hand-coded traceback, oracle inside inference, injected known solver, new
output decoder or human-designed genome repair enters a population.

## Controlled comparison

Use three RNG replicates in two separately reported origin blocks:

- **Carried random origin:** preserve the exact O/P depth9 and depth16 routing
  genotypes, corridor/reachability descendants, and useful original founder
  anchors in a fixed population of96. Build the remaining membership by a
  performance-blind rule frozen before evaluation. Replicates share the routing
  ancestor; they are independent search RNG runs, not independent discoveries
  of that ancestor.
- **Fresh random origin:** draw new96-founder populations from the established
  random-genome generator. Introduce no preserved routing lineage or known
  solver. This track remains a central part of the experiment and may require
  longer cumulative search later; a negative small pilot does not close it.

Within each origin/replicate, copy the exact initial genome pool and RNG state
into two evolutionary arms. Change only the training source-position mixture:
source-at-junction cases versus a mixture containing a one-edge shared prefix.
Use a common paired-correctness/partial-response ranking, mutation, archive,
immigration, elite and protected-diversity policy. Keep genome size/capacity and
all scheduling rules identical. Freeze the exact mixture and batch schedule
before research calls; a candidate starting point is half source-at-junction and
half one-edge-prefix cases versus source-at-junction only, with matched arm
lengths, shapes, poses and common corridor/no-path controls.

Include equal-evaluation random-resampling controls with the same protected
anchors where applicable, plus frozen initial controls. The exact number of
conditions, generations and calls must fit the registered budget using fixture
costs. Any reduction occurs before scientific evaluation and applies symmetrically;
no promising arm receives an extra allowance. Record actual work as well as
calls/time, since equal allowances do not imply equal realized work.

## New data and protection against shortcuts

Exclude all181928 whole layouts through P, including software fixtures and every
pose. Construct new matched graphs with identical downstream geometry as the
source moves from the junction to the first upstream cell. Include all eight
poses in training coverage and validation. Start with small arms and simple
forks; prefix2 and larger/extra-junction/cycle cases can serve as separately
reported transfer tests. These are explicitly P-informed task choices, not a
new general-maze benchmark.

Pair both goals on each layout. Complete correct shortest rasters take priority;
branch spill, source/goal-only output and flooding cannot count as solutions.
Partial credit can provide a gradient but cannot satisfy a success gate. Preserve
source-at-junction/corridor anchors and useful diversity equally in every arm.
Save all populations, RNG, concrete edits, births, raw rasters, archives and
budget counters at matched checkpoints. Never relabel a component-grower or a
forced intermediate frame as a path solver.

Use broader validation with several independent contexts per geometry, a common
rank and fixed budgets. Freeze all final choices and ancestry before generating
an untouched confirmation corpus. The final must compare both pressures and
all controls on exactly the same inputs, and distinguish paired correctness,
individual legality, shortest optimality and disconnected behavior.

## Readiness and stopping

Predeclare thresholds before research calls. A larger run should require at
least two of three search replicates in the same origin block to gain normal
paired shortest solutions at positive prefixes on independent validation,
outperform both the unchanged-curriculum and resampling controls, and retain
at least95% of applicable source-at-junction/corridor anchor performance without
increasing false no-path claims. Require more than a single pose or clutter
context, and confirm those gains on the untouched final. Exact counts and
context requirements belong in the preregistration after fixture construction,
not in a posthoc interpretation of the pilot.

A carried-lineage gain would establish evolvability conditional on an already
found random-origin primitive. A fresh-origin gain would be a distinct discovery
result. Report them separately. A failure should preserve the lineages and
checkpoint diversity and diagnose whether search changed output or only cost;
it should not trigger an unchanged60–90-minute extension.

Static causal transplants/reversions of edit9 remain an optional separate
mechanism study, with engineered variants explicitly separated from random-origin
populations. They are not needed to label P's adjacent-state transition and must
not be silently used to improve this pilot's seeds. No new experiment has been
launched by this proposal.
