# Proposed next step after X-R1: make multigene opportunities measurable

Proposal only, not launched or preregistered. X-R1 is closed at 32 generations;
its activation gate fails in all 18 sexual conditions. Do not extend it, start
the conditional 75-minute efficacy experiment, reuse invalid original X as
scientific evidence, or relabel training data as holdout. Preserve all historical
and new populations, immutable gene identities, ancestry, protected skills and
RNG checkpoints. Exclude all 255,356 whole layouts through the valid restart.

## One bounded question

Can a less restrictive generic linkage rule permit substantial multi-instruction
exchange while protecting immediate regulation, on the same parental opportunities?
Separate that question from whether the current populations contain enough
related, meaningfully different genomes for mating.

The present transitive rule admits a multi-change segment in only 22 carried and
61 fresh linked proposals out of 3,072 each, at their recorded lengths. Even
before closure, only 439 and 333 have such an option. The first number motivates
a linkage change; the second cautions that linkage alone may be insufficient.
The actual donor shortlist often contains no eligible related variant at all.
These are training-conditional descriptions, not causal efficacy estimates.

## Fixed-parent opportunity assay first

Before running more maze evaluations, preregister a small static assay of saved
parent/donor shortlists and inherited identities. Use all scheduled proposals,
including those originally rejected for lack of a compatible donor, rather than
conditioning on accepted splices or high fitness. Report carry and fresh origins
and all three historical replicates separately. Reconstruct exact parent states
from saved events. Repeated parent pairs are correlated; report unique-pair
counts as well as opportunity-weighted results.

Also predeclare a small, fitness-blind sample of other donors from the same
saved populations. This distinguishes an unlucky/restrictive eight-draw
shortlist from a population-wide shortage of related variation. Keep the
sample and static-comparison cap fixed across operator rules; a sampled absence
still cannot prove that no donor exists anywhere. Record how many mutable
families survive alongside the protected anchors.

Compare on exactly the same pairs and common structural pools:

1. Existing full transitive STORE dependency closure.
2. Immediate **gate-writer** linkage: for a STORE with a scratch gate, link it
   to every possible reaching writer of that gate, stopping the backwards scan
   only at an unconditional definition. Derive links from both parents and
   retain the same inherited-locus alignment rules. Do not recursively include
   the writers' operands or all STORE value dependencies. This protects one
   regulatory relation, not a certified whole functional module.
3. Unlinked segments and a matched random-boundary control.

Cross each rule with the current common length pool 2/4/8/16 and a pool that
also permits 32. Apply each length pool to every rule/control; do not attribute
larger-block effects to linkage. Retain common proposal lengths and exact
same-pair random tickets for virtual selection. Count candidate segments that
change at least two rows, not merely the number of instructions copied.
Independently verify groups and homology; bound runtime and artifacts.

Exact d07/d12 may remain a disclosed W/X-informed software fixture, never a
held-out demonstration. Its immediate gate writers at 192/207 plus STORE 222
span 31 positions, whereas full transitive closure spans 220. Those numbers
motivate the factorial length test, not hardcoded exceptions at those loci.
Fresh rules must be inferred only from their own genomes.

This assay uses inspected training histories to design an operator. It provides
no independent maze-solving evidence, creates no promoted champion and requires
zero new organism inference calls. Recalculate opportunities in all saved arms
to show dependence on the earlier policy's population distribution.

## Decision before any new evolution

If immediate linkage still has few opportunities despite a common larger block,
do not spend another long run on the same mating supply. Design an explicit,
matched **related-lineage diversity** mechanism first: retain sibling variants
or a bounded family archive without importing known-solver code. Wholly random
founders and descendants remain a primary track. Any such mechanism must apply
equally to linked, unlinked, sham and mutation controls, preserve a fixed total
population/evaluation budget, and record displacement of other diversity.
Do not assert that the sparse pilot proves that additional generations cannot
increase homologous variation.

If opportunities are sufficient, preregister one fresh short activation pilot
of the chosen rule, with new seeds/layouts, unchanged 10%/eight-generation/
two-of-three criteria unless a different threshold is explicitly justified
before new results. Do not lower the gate retrospectively to admit X-R1.
Include all scheduled attempts in its denominator and verify retained donor
changes after mutation. Keep fresh and carry decisions separate.

Only successful activation should lead to a separately preregistered 60–75-minute
efficacy comparison. Require new long-prefix paired ability, true alternative-cycle
correctness, retained corridor/ordinary skills, no increased false no-path claims
and independent final confirmation, with actual multigene transfer in ancestry.
Recombination ancestry alone cannot distinguish transferred skill from a later
mutation; causal attribution requires matched offspring/parent or replay controls.

General solving, causal multirange advantage and competitive speed remain open.
The next objective is a reproduction design that can test combination of abilities,
not a claim that either sexual reproduction or random-origin discovery has failed.
