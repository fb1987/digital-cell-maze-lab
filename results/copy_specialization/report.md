**Neither mutation arm produced a confirmed additive solver.** Development screening found0/1,024strict additive passes in each arm; fresh confirmation found0/4fixed primary passes per arm. No nominee combines the full parent and child repertoires. Restricting edits to the copied writer and reader also failed to improve preservation consistently:194/1,024local proposals retained every original correct response without false no-path claims, versus209/1,024generic proposals.

## Interpretation and next recommendation

M20 gives no support for this copy-and-reader-local restriction as a solution to the retention barrier. It did not improve retention consistently, and it found no additive variant in either arm. The one new short ordinary route is real fresh-confirmation evidence of additional specialist behaviour, with losses far larger than the gain. Together with M19’s saved histories, the evidence still supports accessible local innovations and persistent tradeoffs; it does not show construction of a general maze algorithm.

This does not rule out larger neutral neighborhoods, more than five edits, sustained adaptation or a different regulatory representation. The current VM can execute the intact solver controls, so this result does not establish a lack of representational capacity. It addresses accessibility under this bounded variation grammar and these inherited scaffolds. More size or time remains a hypothesis, not a consequence of the new data.

I recommend a small causal test of the shared specialist readout before another broad run: compare exact saved specialists with removal/bypass of the copied process and restoration of the original block6terminal, on a prospectively frozen panel containing their new success and representative lost skills. That can establish whether useful copied computation contributed or whether the improvement came from replacing the existing readout. It should be labelled mechanism/development work, preserve every original genome and avoid claiming the reused cases as new confirmation. The result would guide a more precise regulatory design or future search; simply imposing the same local restriction for longer is not supported here.

No such next phase has been launched. Random-origin discovery remains central; these acquired specialists and all unselected proposals remain preserved. No known solver, recombination, cloud compute or generated path-specific answer entered candidate inference.
