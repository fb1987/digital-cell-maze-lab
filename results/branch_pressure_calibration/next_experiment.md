# After V12N test cumulative behavioral variation with full orientation coverage

Proposal only; not launched or preregistered. N is complete and closed. Do not
resume it, extend its caps or train/reselect using its inspected final cases.
Exclude all177,032 whole layouts through N. Preserve all populations, random roots,
mutation events/RNG, neutral children, component growers, founder81 and old solvers.

## Question

Does a short cumulative sequence of heritable changes expose useful output
variation that independent one-edit neighborhoods miss? N found543 changed programs
among576 mutation opportunities, yet only1–5 children per group/topology changed
any queried mask. Work/stopping counters changed in116–128, so outputs can remain
stable while computation changes. This does not establish an inactive genome or
prove that more capacity would help. It motivates testing cumulative variation.

N also omitted quarter-turn poses from calibration. Its final shows a one-sided
positive response from unchanged founder81 only in omitted orientations. Use all
eight poses prospectively. This is broader assay coverage, not permission to
relabel N final cases as validation or select on them. N's zero activation and
failed gate remain the official result; no pressure-only pilot follows it.

## Proposed prospective design

Use all three original M random founder pools, explicitly M/N-informed in design.
Preserve each founder and sample independent mutation walks without fitness-guided
acceptance or known-solver injection. Candidate depths could be0,1,4,16, fixed
before cases and exact cost profiling. Every step is one existing bounded V3 edit;
record all intermediate genomes/events/RNG even if only fixed endpoints are
queried. No exposure mutation; compare local/nonlocal under identical programs.
The chosen depths and number of walks are proposals, not registered parameters.

Allocate equal terminal candidate-input evaluation budgets to cumulative-walk,
independent one-edit and fresh-random-resampling comparisons. Include the unchanged
founder as a common frozen control. Do not call an advantage of a larger evaluated
pool an advantage of edit depth. Preserve parent links for every walk, even when
a step is ineffective or loses old skills. Protected component-grower/founder81
anchors remain unchanged and equally available in later selection experiments.

Use new elementary paired adjacent/two-/three-edge cases with shared prefixes,
all eight orientations, matched corridors and disconnected goals. Save all layouts
and distinguish route-shape types from clutter-seeded contexts. Allocate the same
cases across comparisons; freeze any nominees before independent final generation.
The existing normal-emission runtime and inference budgets remain fixed, so the
initial question is cumulative mutational reachability rather than changed stopping.

Report both signed and positive goal-dependent interior contrasts as diagnostics;
retain complete legal/shortest pairs as the primary solver outcome. Identical
floods and goal-pixel-only switching are zero decision evidence. Negative contrast
is a detectable response in the wrong direction, not progress. Track the first
edit depth where masks, counters and appropriate goal response change, corridor
losses, hidden-work costs and ancestry. Do not infer hidden trajectories from
matching terminal masks or aggregate counters. No forced trace counts as a solution.

Freeze numeric behavioral-supply thresholds and a hard approximately12-minute
candidate cap after actual-size profiling. A promising supply result should
include reproducible normal responses beyond markers, a meaningful depth/control
contrast and useful variants in multiple original founder groups. Do not require
that an entire general solver already exists before allowing a diversity-preserving
search; distinguish a gate for mechanism exploration from the much stronger gate
for claiming solving progress. Conversely, a higher partial scalar alone must not
trigger a60–90minute correctness campaign.

## Decision after the assay

If cumulative edits produce new useful response diversity, propose a short,
matched diversity-preserving evolution comparison with equal budgets and frozen
controls. Retain neutral intermediates and useful corridor/adjacent skills rather
than collapsing immediately to a single partial-score winner. Establish normal
held-out non-adjacent paired gains in at least2/3 evolutionary replicates before
a longer correctness-focused run.

If substantial cumulative changes still affect computation but rarely affect
normal output, investigate a specific expression/emission bottleneck using frozen
representative genomes and independently specified probes. That would be a separate
runtime-mechanism study. Do not silently change stopping, inject a solver or claim
negative pilot evidence rules out random-origin discovery. No such study is launched
by this proposal.
