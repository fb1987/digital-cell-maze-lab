# M6 — sustained upstream continuation: confirmed limited gain, no target breakthrough

M6 completed normally, and both registered audits passed on their first attempts.
The new A0 descendant combines some junction/cycle routing with retained correct
disconnection detection. It does **not** extend routing to the targeted farther
upstream starts, and loses substantial corridor and ordinary-maze ability.
All nine nominee aliases fail the registered upstream, long-ordinary and combined
acquisition criteria. This is a bounded negative result for this search setup,
with one narrower acquired ability worth preserving—not a general solver.

The strongest design lesson is about reproductive opportunity. Of 242,460 mutation
births, **202,560 (83.54%) went to A0's lineage, but only 4,324 (1.78%) to the new
M5 upstream specialist standard0191**. Keeping an exact protected copy did not
ensure sustained evolution around that specialist. This experiment gave much
more evolutionary effort to A0 than to the lineage that motivated the continuation.

## What was run and frozen

Registration `929150f` was pushed before scientific generation. Three populations
of96 shared six exact M5-informed random-origin references. Each began with six
protected copies plus30 clonal slots each of standard0191, A0 and standard0434.
They had independent future RNG histories but shared origins. The unchanged M1
runtime, one generic mutation opportunity per mutated child, seven random
immigrants and45 mutated children per population per round were used throughout.
No known solver, writer-holding extension, mating or output cleanup entered
reproduction. Engineering controls remained outside the populations.

Search stopped at the registered70-minute target after **1,796 matched rounds**,
then completed its final development assessment and froze choices/ancestry/states.
Those exact choices were pushed as `e50da65` before any independent confirmation.
The search audit passed before fresh cases were generated. All old layouts,
including discarded/rejected/unqueried variants, were excluded.

Each history received exactly8,275,968 logical training queries. Development
assessment alias counts were826,336/827,120/827,512 because live/archive/frozen
identity overlap differed. Exact-genotype/query deduplication reduced physical
work. These are equal training allocations across histories, not equal CPU costs
or a controlled comparison against a resampling arm. Frozen initial references
and protected alternatives were evaluated on identical assessment/final inputs.

Nine final nomination roles selected four identities: three unchanged A0 clones
and one mutated descendant occupying two roles. These are **two distinct nominee
genotypes**, not nine independent discoveries. Including the exact parent, roots,
protected references and engineering controls, ten physical models received the
same fresh1,680 inputs each. There was no final-data reselection.

## What the search allocation reveals

All280,176 births were replayed:242,460 one-opportunity mutation births and37,716
fresh random immigrants. The mutation allocation by exact root was:

| Root lineage | Mutation births | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| A0 | 202,560 | 83.54% |
| Original A1 | 18,618 | 7.68% |
| M5 standard0434 | 16,509 | 6.81% |
| M5 standard0191 | 4,324 | 1.78% |
| New random-immigrant lineages | 449 | 0.19% |

By assessment100, standard0191 had only its single protected slot in each live
population, despite starting with31 slots. Some descendants temporarily returned;
this is not a claim of permanent disappearance. At the final round it again had
only the protected slot in all three populations. Its lineage received566,803
and2955 mutation births respectively. Meanwhile A0 occupied71/74/74 final slots,
including its protected copy. The old roots were preserved but generated no
mutation births under this run's parent selection.

This does not prove that a quota or separate island would succeed, or identify
one fitness term as the causal reason for dominance. It does establish that
"1,796 generations" was not1,796 generations of equally sustained exploration
around each starting mechanic. The exact specimens survived; their reproductive
opportunities were very unequal. The generalist A0 ancestor's corridor,
ordinary and disconnection performance provided a strong competing repertoire.
The rigid final gate alone did not enforce reproductive allocation.

Development also understated losses: the child retained all24 A0 pairs on the
fixed392-input development panel, but lost12/96 on fresh confirmation with more
poses/contexts. This is why the final freeze and unseen panel mattered. We cannot
infer that every unselected genotype lacks useful skills; final claims concern
the registered nominees and accompanying fixed references. All other records
remain available for a separately registered future analysis.

## Recommended next step

Do not simply repeat this mixed-population run for longer. Keep random-origin
search central, but first test whether the promising mechanics receive enough
reproductive opportunity. A separately registered, bounded comparison of current
shared selection versus fixed lineage-specific breeding allocations would directly
address the observed imbalance, using the same total mutation/evaluation budget,
exact starting material, runtime, mutation operator and common cases. Keeping
allocation as the changed factor would make the result more interpretable than
simultaneously adding memory, mating and a new curriculum.

Retain the strict combined success criterion, including correct no-path, while
reporting partial specialist advances honestly. Preserve A0, standard0191,
standard0434 and this new descendant; none is a replacement for all the others.
Audit actual births per lineage during that future test, not just the survival
of protected specimens. Freeze new candidates before fresh confirmation as usual.
This is a proposal only: no next experiment or additional cases have been launched.
M6 narrows the search-design problem; it does not settle whether local regulatory
systems can evolve a general solver.
