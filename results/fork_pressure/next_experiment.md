# Recommendation after V12G — test robust routing before another long search

Status: **proposal only; not launched or preregistered**. V12G's fixed 720-second
search and its confirmation are complete. Do not resume or extend that budget.
Preserve all 30 populations/RNG, anchors, pair/choice/corridor archives and the
random-resampling controls. Random-origin discovery remains central.

The new positive evidence is stronger than a signalling proxy: one preserved
lineage completes both goal choices on 16 small bases in all eight poses, including
four longer pairs up to 9/9 edges. Its successes survive pulse removal and nonlocal
zeroing. Static goal flags and endpoint halting remain. A68-gene random draw also
solves four bases in all poses. Neither a multirange necessity nor a consistent
fresh-evolution advantage has been established. No cohort passes the preregistered
larger-run gate, and tiny-task outputs remain much slower than compiled BFS.

The next bounded study should resolve two questions before spending another hour:

1. **Mechanism and transfer of fixed variants.** Freeze an explicit panel of all
   V12G primary selections and their frozen/resampling controls; keep the best
   lineage illustrative rather than selecting additional genomes on final data.
   Confirm on substantially more independent bases, with longer goal distances,
   a second decision junction, and cycles providing genuinely alternative
   source-to-goal routes. Keep matched corridor controls. Test goal-flag and
   endpoint-halt dependence separately, with production parity and intact/damaged
   controls. Do not treat pulse removal as removal of all goal information.
2. **Validation breadth.** V12G's four independent validation bases per replicate
   let one large validation gain become a transfer loss. Before new breeding,
   freeze the existing validation-nominated genome pool and compare narrow versus
   broader validation-based selection on entirely new validation cases, followed
   by another untouched final set. This can test selection robustness without
   changing mutation/capacity or granting extra evolutionary compute to one arm.

A new protocol must choose the exact primary question, seeds, base counts,
fixed inference budget and continuation criterion before candidate evaluation;
the two ideas above are not authorization to bundle unlimited assays. A modest
confirmation/selection study is preferable to an unchanged 60–90-minute run now.
If useful paired routing repeats across independent lineages and the broader
selection improves transfer, a larger matched evolutionary run is then justified.
Continue testing both inherited and fresh random origins, with a resampling
control, while preserving precursors and neutral diversity.

Exclude **all 136,536 whole layouts** through V12G. Do not retune against any
inspected G final cases. The fresh route shapes remain a small induced-fork
grammar: dangling cycles do not demonstrate shortest-path competition. Any new
cycle claim must include alternative routes of unequal lengths and independent
legality/optimality checks. Keep CPU time, emitted outputs, work and memory
separate from partial choice signals, and keep known solvers outside random
lineages.
