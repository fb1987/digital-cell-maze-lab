Proposal only; not registered or launched. Do not rerun/extend V13A, resume Z
silently, train/reselect on inspected final cases, or replace preserved specialists.
Exclude all275168 whole layouts through V13A plus later fixtures.

The nine new Z selections do not hide a correct path later in their failed traced
trajectories. Extra output grows or repeats. Yet the three ordinary-mutation
histories acquire elementary goal pairs absent from their exact roots, through
both pulse-based and flag-based mechanisms. Existing specialists solve upstream
pairs. Random-origin discovery stays central; time alone inside the current
reactions is not the next intervention to test.

reward reducing off-route output without losing the complete source-goal route,
on goal-paired one-/two-cell-prefix tasks. The current fitness already penalizes
branches. Before registration, inspect its actual ranking and demonstrate how
any proposed objective differs; synthetic complete/partial/empty/flooded masks
must prevent rewarding empty outputs or a route-containing flood. These are
fitness checks, never an inference-time cleanup algorithm.

Calibrate a positive control separately to establish that the runtime and budget
can express/refine suppression. Do not inject it into fresh or carried lineages.

versus the single revised pressure from matched pools, retaining frozen and random
resampling controls. Preserve fresh random founders as a separate track, and
label any Z-derived populations explicitly as continued random-origin ancestry.

corridor/goal-choice diversity fixed. Do not combine the test with recombination,
another architecture change, or a larger capacity change.
