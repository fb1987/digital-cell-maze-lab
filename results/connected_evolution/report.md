## Reviewed interpretation

The negative endpoint is not merely an artifact of the strict retention threshold. Every nominee also has zero new successful individual inputs beyond the protected union before applying that threshold. The gate is false because the nominated genomes have zero M19 mutation births, even though they retain all of their own root and phase-entry skills. Their mask, status and step count match their exact root and entry on all 1,680 inputs. The 27 roles reduce to three inherited genotypes: anchor/0 occupies all nine integrated and nine ordinary roles; shared_child occupies the three trio-0 exploratory roles; gated_specialist occupies the other six. These are aliases of existing algorithms, not 27 independent findings.

The nine primary nominees each solve 96/400 complete paired tasks, 200/200 corridors, 17/256 ordinary mazes in both poses and 168/168 disconnected inputs, with zero false no-path claims. Ordinary success is 6/64 perfect 11×11, 5/64 perfect 15×15, 3/64 random 9×9 and 3/64 random 15×15. Their longest correct ordinary route is 11 edges; all three nominee genotypes solve zero of the 80 ordinary bases with routes of at least 16 edges in both poses. The other two nominees' longest correct ordinary routes are 6 and 5 edges. Correctness on short routes and corridors is real, but this remains far from a general solver.

The preserved M15 specialist still demonstrates an inherited tradeoff on this same panel. It solves 184 paired tasks and 200 corridors versus its actual parent's 128 and 166. It retains all the parent's complete paired tasks and adds 56, including the previously discovered 16 nonfork prefix-2+ pairs beyond the other protected references. It loses three of the parent's 11 ordinary successes and all 168 correct disconnection responses, while making zero false no-path claims. All of this ability was already in the protected starting material. The 16 upstream pairs cannot count as an M19 acquisition. Different ordinary totals in older experiments used different cases and are not evidence of gains or losses here. Exact saved comparisons are in report_review_evidence.json.

### What duplication actually achieved

Connected duplication was neither absent nor immediately eliminated. Across its three populations, 9,429 prospective copy opportunities led to 7,101 initialized connected copies and 2,328 ordinary fallbacks. There were 193,578 eligible-edge presentations across those opportunities; these are repeated choices, not unique edges. Of 7,077 connected-copy children with a subsequent training exposure, 1,664 survived that selection (23.5%). Disconnected copying produced 7,412 copies from 9,352 opportunities, with 1,940 fallbacks; 1,725/7,383 next-exposed children survived (23.4%). These related, selected births do not provide an independent significance test or establish equal operator quality.

At the end, 42/51/34 connected-arm identities in the deduplicated live-plus-archive pools had a copy in their M19 ancestry: 127 total. Disconnected pools retained 47/33/42 such identities: 122 total. A copy ancestor does not guarantee that the copied process is still present, different from its donor or causally useful. Connected populations also recorded 133 spare deactivations, versus 144 disconnected and 229 generic. Spare recycling was available and used; no ending lineage had more than two initialized-copy births in its ancestry. All 2,514 genotype no-ops were charged.

The deepest M19 mutation chains still present in the final connected pools are 9/16/12 births, compared with generic 11/13/14 and disconnected 18/7/10. These are final live/archive depths, not maxima over every discarded historical organism. Thus 275 population rounds did not mean 275 cumulative mutations in a surviving solver lineage. Random-origin exploration also continued through 17,325 fresh immigrants; 111,375 mutant births were produced overall. None of the confirmed nominees descended through a new M19 mutation. We have demonstrated accessible heritable copying and persistence, but have not demonstrated useful differentiation followed by cumulative skill integration.

M18 already showed that initialized attachment can preserve a process and affect an existing reader. M19 tests the next link and gives a negative result: this implementation did not turn that opportunity into a confirmed improvement within its registered horizon. It does not identify whether the limiting cause is variation, phenotype quality, selection pressure, the fixed banks, state capacity or insufficient evolutionary depth. All arms shared the selector, banks and inference limits, so those explanations remain hypotheses. Nor does this confirmation cover every unselected descendant in the journal; the defensible capability conclusion concerns the frozen nominees.

### Recommended next step — proposal only

I recommend a bounded analysis of the saved M19 histories before another longer evolution run. Follow copy-bearing descendants from their first training exposure: separate changed output behaviour from extra inactive or redundant state, measure new correct cases and losses against parents and protected references wherever they were evaluated on the same panel, and track subsequent survival and breeding. Mark unavailable same-panel comparisons as missing rather than inferring them from different panels. Include generic and disconnected histories under the same analysis, and distinguish reserved exploration births from shared-lexicase births. This can test whether potentially useful changes were generated and then discarded, or whether surviving changes seldom improve routing at all.

If that analysis locates useful intermediates being lost, a separately registered search-policy change would have a concrete target. If it mostly finds redundant copies or destructive changes, first investigate a bounded variation/representation change with an exact old-behaviour embedding and explicit cost accounting. Keep final correctness, disconnection and unseen longer-route acceptance fixed, and preserve random-origin provenance. More time and capacity remain testable hypotheses; M19 does not yet justify a much larger unchanged run. This recommendation is not authorization or a launch: no next phase, additional confirmation, recombination, known solver or cloud compute has been started.
